Haeduri Chicken is a Korean restaurant at 6 Young St, Southport. Come in to dine, grab takeaway, or book a table ahead, and expect to want more than one dish. Guests say every table ends up with two or three, and it's easy to spot something else you want to try while you wait.

Regulars love the Tongdak, a whole fried chicken that one reviewer calls an old school Korean dish you can't find elsewhere in Australia. The tteokbokki hot pot and spicy chicken feet hot pot are also favourites. Guests praise the friendly staff even on busy nights, which has earned a 4.9 rating from 432 reviews. As one put it: come and try chicken and beer, you will not regret it.
