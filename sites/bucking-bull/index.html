Low and Slow

Low and slow is our mantra. A medley of slow-cooked meats sits at the centre of everything we do, cooked for anywhere up to 12 hours so every roast that comes out of the oven is tender and tasty.

We challenge the idea that roasts are just for Sundays. We believe roasts should be enjoyed every day of the week.

Australian Born, Australian Grown

All of our roast meats are 100% Australian grown. We wouldn't have it any other way! We work with some of the country's leading suppliers to find the nation's finest beef, pork and lamb.

  • Pork: grown on a network of farms in the Riverina region of southern New South Wales and Victoria. Our roast pork and pork belly are slow cooked, with juicy meat under a layer of bubbly crackling.
  • Lamb: premium grass-fed, pasture-raised lamb from southern NSW and Victoria.

Catering

Bring Bucking Bull roast meats to your next party, corporate function, meeting, Christmas party, family gathering or even your wedding. Roast catering is available from our Toowoomba restaurant.

Recreate the ultimate roast dinner with roast beef, roast pork or pork belly, plus sides including roast vegetables, steamed vegetables, potato bake and classic roast potatoes. Don't forget the pork crackle and gravy!

Going more casual? We also offer breakfast and sandwich platters, and our Make-Your-Own Hot Roast Sub Packs. We supply our signature sub rolls, slices of slow-cooked beef or pork and our gravy. You do the rest, and you get to take the credit.

Good to know

  • Notice of 3 business days before pick up is required, so we can slow cook your meats overnight.
  • There is no minimum order spend.
  • Catering is pick up only. Delivery isn't available for catering orders.
  • Your order can be hot or cold. Reheating instructions are provided with every catering order.
  • Once you order, you'll receive a confirmation email. The store will then phone you to confirm your order, the pickup date and time, and take payment over the phone or in store.
  • We can't provide staff or equipment for your function, but we're happy to advise on how to make your catering a success.
  • You're always welcome to contact the store first to talk through your event.
