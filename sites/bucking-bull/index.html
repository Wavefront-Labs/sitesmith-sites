Now you can enjoy the flavour and quality of Bucking Bull’s Roast Meats at your next event! We offer a range of catering packages and products to impress your guests, whether you’re organising a party, corporate function, meeting, Christmas party or even a wedding.

Recreate the ultimate roast dinner from our catering menu including roast beef, roast pork or pork belly and a range of sides including roast vegetables, steamed vegetables, potato bake and classic roast potatoes. Don’t forget the pork crackle and gravy!

Want to ditch the knife and fork for a more casual event? We also offer a range of breakfast and sandwich platters to truly satisfy your guests. You can even recreate our famous roast meat and gravy subs with our ‘Make-Your-Own Hot Roast Sub Packs. We provide you with our signature sub rolls, slices of slow cooked beef or pork and mouth-watering gravy and you do the rest (and get to take the credit!)

How do I place a catering order?

  • Complete the catering order form and press submit. You will also receive a confirmation email with a summary of your order.
  • The selected store will be in touch by phone to confirm your order and take payment. We will also confirm the pickup date and time.

How do I know if my order has gone through?

Upon placing your catering order, you will receive an email confirming the items ordered, as well as a phone call from the store confirming your order details. Payment over the phone or instore is required.

What kind of functions or events would Bucking Bull catering be suitable for?

Our wide menu has the flexibility to cater for everything from your office meeting or breakfast, through to a 21st birthday party or office Christmas party.

You are always welcome to contact the store first to discuss your event and requirements before an online order is placed.

Find your closest Bucking Bull Store.

How much notice is required?

We require notice of 3 business days before pick up – this allows us time to prepare and slow cook your delicious meats overnight.

Is there a minimum order amount?

There is no minimum order spend.

Is delivery available?

Unfortunately delivery is not available for catering. The order must be collected from the store.

Will my order be hot or cold?

It’s up to you! You can advise us as to how you would like to pick up your catering order. We can arrange for the catering to be hot or cold, and will also issue you with reheating instructions should you wish to reheat your order.

How do I reheat my catering order?

Reheating instructions are provided with each catering order.

Can your staff serve at my function, or can you supply the necessary equipment?

Unfortunately we are unable to provide staff or equipment to be used at the function. We can however consult with you and advise on the best way to ensure that your Bucking Bull catering is a success.

Discover

Nobody knows roasts quite like Bucking Bull!

We’ve been perfecting our basting, roasting and carving methods over the last 18 years and have built what you’d call a healthy obsession with perfection.

With stores in Queensland and Western Australia, our unique brand of soul food has well and truly struck a chord with hungry Aussies. We’re fast becoming Australia’s favourite boutique carvery. Heavenly roast meals? Tick! Satisfying sandwiches, subs and burgers? Uh, duh!

We live for empty plates and full bellies. We combine these traditional cooking methods with contemporary flavours and the result is pretty darn delicious! Grab a napkin, this could get messy!

Australian Born, Australian Grown

All of our roast meats are 100% Australian grown.

Pork Bucking Bull’s pork is grown on a network of farms in Australia’s beautiful Riverina region in southern New South Wales and Victoria. Our famous roast pork and pork belly products are always slow cooked to perfection, with juicy, succulent meat hiding beneath a layer of bubbly crackling.

Lamb Premium grass fed, pasture raised lamb is sourced from Southern NSW and Victoria. Masters OF MEAT Whether slow-roasted or straight from the grill, our menu is unapologetically full of meaty goodness! They key to serving the best slow-cooked meats in Australia lies in great quality produce. We work with some of the country’s leading suppliers to find the nation’s finest beef, pork and lamb products. Oh and in case you were wondering, yes, all of our roast meats are 100% Australian grown. (We wouldn’t have it any other way!)

Own Your Own

You’ve met our roast experts, you’ve seen them wield a carving knife and now you’d fancy being the boss behind the bull yourself. A Bucking Bull franchise gives you the freedom to be your own boss with the tools and resources to continue to grow your business. If you’ve got a hunger for a healthy bottom line and a desire to take control of your career, owning a Bucking Bull could be the opportunity you’ve been looking for.

We’re steadily expanding through Australia and are recruiting passionate and driven franchise partners to join us. If you’re ready to combine your love for quality food with your hunger for success, get in touch!

Bucking Bull is the country’s most loved boutique carvery franchise, with stores around Australia. We are the only retail food concept centred around a genuine love for quality meats. We have been carefully cultivating and refining our techniques to bring customers a truly unique dining experience.

Since 1999 Bucking Bull has developed and nurtured a franchise system that is robust and recognised as one of Australia’s best. Franchisees benefit from an award winning Best Practice model with ongoing coaching and assistance in sales techniques, operations, local area marketing, cost control, purchasing and financial management.

Why else do our franchise partners choose Bucking Bull? They live, breathe, and love to eat our product! From fans and now to franchisees, they are passionate about delivering quality product and the Bucking Bull experience to our customers.

Some Bucking Bull Franchisees had never stepped foot in a commercial kitchen, let alone run a successful food business before. The Bucking Bull team provides comprehensive training and ongoing support including store development, operations, training, marketing and sales techniques as well as how to prepare food that is of consistently high quality.

From the moment you come onboard, we’ll work with you, lending our experience and expertise to every facet of your new business. We have a very hands-on approach to training to help you lead your team of roast experts to glory!

As part of your extensive training you’ll learn:

  • How to deliver high product quality at lower cost
  • How to recruit 12 key performance indicators to tweak and grow your profits
  • Food merchandising secrets to maximise your sales
  • How to keep your customers delighted and fiercely loyal

Franchisees are provided with classroom based training at Bucking Bull’s Support Office, followed by in-store operational training. We also continue to provide ongoing support and mentoring through our best practice system.

Frequently Asked

Have some questions? We’re so glad you asked!

After successfully managing Bucking Bull franchises for over twenty years we think we have all the answers! If you can’t find the answer to your question below, it won’t be far away. Either complete the Enquiry Form below or contact us .

What skills do I need to become a successful Bucking Bull Franchisee?

Our franchise owners come from all walks of life, and don’t necessarily have food retail or restaurant experience. We are looking for individuals who are genuinely driven to succeed in their business.

Retail management or food service experience is desirable, but not essential. The comprehensive training that you will receive will equip you with all the knowledge and skills required for operating your own Bucking Bull franchise.

What is the initial investment?

The cost of a Bucking Bull franchise varies from project to project and depends on the individual store requirements. Generally complete franchises will cost between $150,000 – $400,000.

This initial investment comprises of the franchise fee, intellectual property, store design, fixtures & fittings, equipment, operating systems and all training.

What are the ongoing costs?

Bucking Bull’s franchise fee is 8% of net sales. This includes marketing, IT and operational support.

What is a Franchise Agreement and what is the term?

The Franchise Agreement is awarded to qualified candidates and it is a legally binding contract between franchisee and franchisor. The Agreement will outline the way in which your franchised outlet will work within the Bucking Bull franchise system and has an initial term of 5 + 5 years.

How long will it take to open my Bucking Bull store?

Franchisees typically open their Bucking Bull store within 14 weeks after signing the Franchise Agreement. Certain factors may affect the length of time required to open your store. These factors include locating and securing a satisfactory site, obtaining adequate financing to complete necessary construction or remodeling and obtaining necessary ordinance and or building code permits, licensing and approvals.

What training and support will I get?

The owner-operator is provided with classroom-based training at Bucking Bull’s Support Office, followed by in-store operational training. We assist the franchisee and his/her management team with hiring and provide a “hands-on” support team for the period prior to opening, to post-opening operations support

Are franchisees expected to work in the store?

As part of the award-winning franchise model at Bucking Bull, we do expect franchisees to actively work in their stores. Our experience demonstrates that stores become more successful when franchisees are active in the day to day running of their business.
