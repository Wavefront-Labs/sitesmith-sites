Latitude 30 sits on Coffs Harbour's breathtaking coastline at the International Marina. We pair a relaxed restaurant vibe and sprawling South Pacific and marina views with locally sourced, seasonal ingredients to create a truly memorable dining experience.

Choose relaxed deck seating and watch whales frolicking in the distance, a private dining area overlooking the marina, or a more intimate table with ocean views. Our menu highlights fresh local produce cooked simply, so the food speaks for itself. Enjoy it with a glass of your favourite wine, beer or cocktail, served by our friendly team.

Hours and Bookings

We're open 7 days from 11am to 10pm for lunch and dinner. We're closed Christmas Day and New Years Day.

Kitchen service runs from 11.30am to 8.15pm, with small bites between 3pm and 5pm. Close times may vary slightly on Sundays and public holidays.

Making a reservation

  • Bookings for 1 to 10 diners must be made online.
  • If the time slot you want isn't showing, we're fully booked at that time. Please try another time or day.
  • For groups of 11 or more, please complete the enquiry form and we will be in touch. For short notice, call (02) 6651 6888.
  • Children and infants must be included in your reservation numbers.
  • Please give at least 24 hours notice of any change in numbers or cancellations.

Cancellation policy

Credit card details are required to secure all reservations, and a fee of $25 per seat is held. If a booking is reduced or cancelled within 24 hours of arrival, or the party doesn't turn up, a cancellation charge of $25 per person may apply. To change your booking and avoid fees, call (02) 6651 6888 or email info@latitude30.com.au.

We don't encourage bringing outside food into the restaurant. Celebrating? Our desserts can come with a candle.

Groups and Private Dining

Host your special occasion at Latitude 30, with marina and mountain views on one side and the Pacific Ocean on the other. We welcome weddings, birthdays, showers and whatever else you're celebrating.

  • At peak times, and for Friday to Sunday lunch and dinner, a 3 course menu will apply.
  • Seafood platters and specific menu items can be added to group menus, POA.
  • Beverages are charged on consumption and can be restricted to help manage your budget.
  • Final numbers must be advised 24 hours before your arrival.

Semi private dining area

Our semi private marina side dining area has spectacular marina and mountain views and seats up to 40. It's perfect for a corporate event or a small wedding celebration with family and friends. Group dining menus apply, and a room hire fee may apply for fewer than 40 guests.

You're welcome to decorate the room to your liking, and a screen and projector are available on request. Complete the enquiry form and we will be in touch.

Gift Vouchers

An unforgettable dining experience at Latitude 30 makes a fabulous gift. E-gift vouchers start from $50.

Choose a value, then add the recipient's email address and a personalised message. The voucher is sent instantly, and the recipient simply shows the code on their phone when paying.

  • Valid for three years from the date of purchase.
  • Please treat vouchers like cash. They cannot be reissued if lost or stolen.
  • Vouchers are not exchangeable for cash.
