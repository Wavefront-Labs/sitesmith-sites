Ocean Front Restaurant Coffs Harbour

Latitude 30

Sitting on Coffs Harbours breathtaking coast line, Latitude 30 combines a relaxed restaurant vibe, sprawling South Pacific and marina views and locally sourced seasonal ingredients to create a truly memorable dining experience.

From relaxed deck seating watching whales frolicking in the distance to a private dining area overlooking the marina to a more intimate dining experience with ocean views, enjoy delicious dishes bursting with flavour alongside a glass of your favourite wine, beer or cocktail served by our friendly team.

Fresh tasty food, friendly service, relaxed dining with amazing views.

Our Menus

Our menu highlights incredible, fresh local produce cooked simply allowing the food to speak for itself and our chefs to create unforgettable dishes, bursting with flavour.

For Groups

Host your special occasion at Latitude 30, with a breathtaking location of marina and mountain views one side and Pacific Ocean views the other. Weddings, birthdays, showers – whatever you’re celebrating.

Vouchers

Gift Vouchers

An unforgettable dining experience at Latitude 30, what a fabulous gift idea!

Vouchers start from $50 upwards and to purchase an e-gift voucher, simply click on the PURCHASE VOUCHER button, nominate a value and move through the online purchasing process including the email address of the recipient and a personalised message. The e-gift voucher will be sent instantly and is easily redeemable by the recipient showing their e-gift voucher code on their phone upon payment.

Gift vouchers are valid for three years from the date of purchase and must be used within this time period. We ask that e-gift vouchers are treated like cash, they should not be reissued if lost or stolen and gift vouchers are not exchangeable for cash.
