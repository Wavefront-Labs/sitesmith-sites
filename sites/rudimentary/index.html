Who we are

Rudimentary is a cafe and community space that aims to contribute to the transformation and evolution of a vibrant and culturally rich part of Melbourne. The block had been in the family for over a decade but had never been more than an unofficial car park. With a limited budget and the need to build from scratch, we set out to create a business that was simple and could be adapted for future use.

The name comes from the idea that simple building blocks, whether fresh produce, shipping containers or recycled materials, can be used to create something more than the sum of their parts. It was chosen early on and shaped our whole approach to the business and the design.

The building

The building is made from three 40ft high cube shipping containers and one 20ft container, and was designed to be relocated if the need arises. In theory it could become an identical business or a home in the country. We chose containers for their ease of transport, robust character and simplicity. They're also synonymous with the West, with the ports of Melbourne only a stone's throw away.

Sustainability

Sustainability has been a key focus of the build and the business from the very beginning. Some of the features:

  • Sustainable materials
  • Rainwater capture, with a 20,000L tank collecting water from the roof for the plants
  • Passive cooling and heating
  • Double-glazing and insulation
  • A building designed to be relocated
  • Composting out the back, where coffee grounds and kitchen garden scraps become compost for the garden

The garden

The garden ties the whole place together. It was designed as a reprieve from the chaos of the surrounding area, a place of relaxation and wonder that also provides for the kitchen. Our love of climbing and hiking meant we wanted to bring a bit of the country back to this little block, which is why we've planted native flora. Herbs, vegetables, fruit trees and edibles grow throughout, especially in the kitchen garden at the rear, which gives our chefs room to be creative.
