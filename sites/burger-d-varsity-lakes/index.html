About Us

about BURGER'D

Burger’d is about delicious food served fresh. We set out to reimagine the traditional takeaway burger — creating wholesome, handcrafted burgers packed with flavour and signature sauces to choose from. Burger’d is about people. Real people, crafting authentic burgers made to order. No fuss. Just fresh ingredients, delivered consistently every time you walk through our doors.

BORN ON THE GOLD COAST

Burger’d started on the Gold Coast with a simple goal — serve proper burgers, made fresh and done right.

We focused on the fundamentals: quality ingredients, handcrafted burgers, and bold flavours that kept people coming back.

What began as a small local concept quickly gained momentum. Word spread, cravings grew, and Burger’d became part of the Gold Coast food scene.

GROWING ACROSS QUEENSLAND

Today, Burger’d has expanded across the Gold Coast and Brisbane.

With 11 locations — and more in the pipeline — we continue to grow while staying true to what got us here.

The grills are still hot. The flavours are still bold. The standards are still high.

And this is only the beginning.

our philosophy

We started Burger’d with one clear idea: to turn the traditional takeaway burger on its head. By combining wholesome ingredients with a twist of flavour-packed sauces and premium meats, we created a burger experience that feels familiar — but tastes next level.

Consistency and quality sit at the heart of everything we do. Whether you’re grabbing a quick bite or sitting down to enjoy the full experience, you’ll always know what to expect: fresh food, done properly.
