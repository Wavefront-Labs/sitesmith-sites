About Burger'd

Born on the Gold Coast

Burger'd started on the Gold Coast with a simple goal: serve proper burgers, made fresh and done right. We focused on the fundamentals of quality ingredients, handcrafted burgers and bold flavours that kept people coming back. What began as a small local concept quickly gained momentum, and Burger'd became part of the Gold Coast food scene.

Growing across Queensland

Today Burger'd has 11 locations across the Gold Coast and Brisbane, with more in the pipeline. The grills are still hot, the flavours are still bold and the standards are still high.

Our philosophy

We set out to turn the traditional takeaway burger on its head. We combine wholesome ingredients and premium meats with flavour-packed sauces to make a burger that feels familiar but tastes next level. Whether you're grabbing a quick bite or sitting down for the full experience, you'll always know what to expect: fresh food, done properly.

What makes us different

  • Bold flavours: from our signature sauces to our flavour twists, we focus on combinations that stand out.
  • Fresh and handcrafted: from grill to bun, your burger is made the moment you order it.
  • Real people: the ones who prep, grill and build every burger with intention.
  • Locally born, still local: born on the Gold Coast, growing but staying true to our roots.

Allergens and Dietary Needs

We take care when preparing your food, but our kitchen handles a variety of ingredients, so cross-contamination may occur. If you have a food allergy or a specific dietary concern, please tell our staff before you order.

  • Nut free cannot be guaranteed, as anything could contain traces.
  • Any item that is deep fried is not gluten free.
