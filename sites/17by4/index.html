17by4 is named after our childhood home in New Delhi: house number 4 on block 17. Four generations lived under that one roof, a joint family of 19 people, and it was full of beautiful people, memories, love, laughter and food (lots of food). This is now our home away from home, where we'd like to recreate those memories with you.

We believe food is more than sustenance. It's an experience. So we keep things warm and welcoming, with delicious comfort food to share with friends and family. Our head chef, David, has years of experience and loves bringing joy to our customers, and he's always experimenting with new recipes and techniques.

All Day at 17by4

We're a fully licensed cafe in central Geelong, open 7 days a week, 365 days a year. You'll find us in the old courthouse building at 40 Gheringhap Street, with indoor and outdoor seating.

  • All day breakfast: fresh pastries and expertly brewed coffee in our cosy indoor seating area
  • Lunch and dinner: chef-inspired dishes indoors or out on our outdoor patio
  • Dogs welcome on the outdoor deck. Puppacinos and doggy treats are always on the house
  • Wheelchair and pram accessible, indoors and out
  • Dine in, takeaway and delivery

High Tea

High tea is served every day at 17by4.

Adults High Tea, $49 per person

When you arrive, choose a drink from over 20 specialty teas, hot chocolate or coffee. While you relax and settle in, a warm soup shooter comes out as your second course. Then comes a three-tier selection of savoury canapés, ribbon sandwiches, petite fours, and scones with jam and cream.

Gluten free, vegetarian and vegan options are available ($5 surcharge applies).

Kids High Tea

Our kids high tea means the whole family can enjoy the experience. It suits kids under 12. Kids choose a drink from hot or iced chocolate, milkshake or soft drink, followed by a three-tier selection where classic favourites get a whimsical twist. It's a very popular choice for kids' birthday parties.

Bookings

We recommend booking high tea at least 24 hours ahead. Call us on 03 5224 1588 or book online. High tea is also popular for birthdays, bridal showers, baby showers and corporate gatherings, so get in touch about availability.

Functions

Our function space sits in a beautiful heritage-listed building in the heart of Geelong's CBD, where historic character meets modern style. It's ideal for small to medium-sized functions. Our team will work with you to make the day personal, relaxed and stress-free, with clear, upfront pricing and no hidden costs.

  • Birthdays, engagements, anniversaries and special milestones
  • Corporate events, meetings and workshops
  • Small weddings and celebrations
  • Social, networking and community events
  • High teas and special gatherings

We offer food and drink packages to suit different occasions and budgets, from relaxed grazing and cocktail-style events to sit-down meals. You're welcome to add freestanding decorations and styling. Functions are available seven days a week with flexible booking times.

Corporate Events

Our central venue is set up for meetings, workshops and training, team-building, networking events and corporate celebrations. We can arrange a boardroom-style setup, theatre seating or a casual layout for networking.

  • Catering packages including light refreshments, high tea and formal dining
  • Layout and decor tailored to your branding or event theme
  • Accessible for guests with mobility needs
  • A dedicated team to support you from start to finish

Catering

We're a small family-owned business offering takeaway and boxed catering for events of all sizes, from corporate gatherings to birthdays and special milestones. Our varied menu suits every taste and dietary preference, at competitive prices.

Pickup and delivery are both available. Get in touch to talk about your event.
