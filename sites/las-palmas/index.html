Las Palmas is a Mexican restaurant on West Esplanade in Manly, with a casual beachside mood and outdoor seating. Guests say it feels like stepping into Mexico, with music, setting and views that carry you away.

Come in for tacos, chips with salsa or the four-dip sampler, which one guest called phenomenal. Service is quick and friendly, and you can dine in or book ahead. Las Palmas is part of The Boathouse Group, a family-owned collection of waterside venues across NSW.

About The Boathouse Group

The Boathouse began in 2008 with a single waterside café in Palm Beach, NSW. Today the Group spans a growing collection of cafés, restaurants, event spaces, accommodation and retail across the Central Coast, Northern Beaches and the South Coast.

It is still family-owned and operated by brothers Ben and Jono Isaac. The Group is built on a commitment to warm hospitality, thoughtful design and memorable experiences.
