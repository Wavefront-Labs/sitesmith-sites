About

  • Dine in

Inspired by the bustling tabernas of Lisbon, Bar Lina is a cosy, fire-fuelled Iberian restaurant serving bold Portuguese and Basque flavours with an Australian wine bar feel. The food revolves around the flame: smouldering embers, blistered veg, and snacky plates that riff on the flavours of Portugal and the Basque coast.

You'll find us in Globe Lane, next door to Restaurant Santino in the heart of Wollongong. The wine leans Iberian and natural, and the soundtrack slides from the groove of Prince to the head-nodding beats of Biggie. Expect stacks of vinyl, buttery lighting, and a room that feels as good as it sounds. Bar Lina is about good food, good wine, and music that moves the room.

What's On

The Good Hour: every Saturday and Sunday, 4-5:30pm

Good drinks. Good tunes. Good times. The Good Hour is our weekend aperitivo session. Settle in for a few drinks, a few plates and a soundtrack made for stretching the afternoon out a little longer. Come for a quick one, order a few things for the table and see where the afternoon takes you. Bookings are available.

  • $5 oysters, gildas, shishito peppers, jamón and fish sando
  • $8 vermouth
  • $12 house wines, martinis and Aperol spritz

Asado Lunches: every Saturday and Sunday lunch

A proper Iberian-style feast built around a whole suckling pig, roasted in our wood-fired oven for 4 hours until the skin crackles and the meat is beautifully tender. It's served with roast potatoes, seasonal vegetables and fresh salad. Best enjoyed like a proper lunch should be: with friends, good wine and time to spare.

  • Whole suckling pig + trimmings: $700
  • Half suckling pig + trimmings: $350
  • 7 days notice required
  • Available Saturday and Sunday lunch

Loose Change: every Thursday from 16th April

Small plates coming in hot, nothing over $14, sangria flowing, sharp pours, and a weekly bottle on offer with a DJ on all night. It's the kind of night where one turns into a few, and staying late feels like the right move.

Valentine's Day: Saturday 14th February 2026

Spoil your loved one with our $79 set menu of beautifully crafted, flame-kissed small plates made for sharing, bursting with bold Iberian flavours.

Mother's Day: Sunday 10th May 2026

We're serving a 5 course shared lunch showcasing a mix of our signature dishes and some new favourites we've been working on. It's all about great flavours, a relaxed atmosphere, and making Mum feel properly looked after. Good food, great wine, and no one doing the dishes.

Father's Day: Sunday 6th September 2026

We're serving a 600g bone-in Black Onyx ribeye from Rangers Valley, MB3+, char-grilled and rested, finished with a Madeira jus and blistered peppers. It's built to share, with hand-cut patatas bravas and witlof salad on the side. There's a complimentary Sagres beer for all dads on the day, and we've got the kids covered too. Good food, cold beer, and no one doing the dishes.

Bookings

Book online for up to 14 guests. For groups of 15 or more, please contact our team to make your booking. Bookings of 7 or more guests dine from our set menu.

We love welcoming little ones. Our restaurant is a cosy, intimate space, so fitting in prams and high chairs can sometimes be a little tricky. If you're bringing the kids, please get in touch before booking so we can chat through the options and see how we can make it work.

For dietary requirements or special requests, please contact us directly and we'll do our best to accommodate. If your preferred time isn't available online, call us on (02) 4225 3500 or email eat@barlina.com.au. We're sorry that our venue is not wheelchair accessible.

  • Bookings for 2 or 3 people have a seating time of 1 hour 45 minutes. Bookings for 4 or more have a seating time of 2 hours.
  • Credit card details are required to secure all bookings. Charges only apply if you cancel within 24 hours of your booking, don't arrive, or reduce numbers on the day.
  • We may contact you about your reservation, so please make sure your email and phone number are up to date.
