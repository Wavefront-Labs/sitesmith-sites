Set over the water on the shores of Lake Wendouree, The Yacht Club is a locally owned cafe serving relaxed breakfasts, leisurely lunches, shared plates, house-made sweets and exceptional coffee, open 7 days a week. Pair it all with a handpicked selection of local wines, beers and cocktails.

We pride ourselves on great food, great wines, a friendly crew, ample parking and the best waterside views in Ballarat. In the warmer months we feature live music on our outdoor deck.

Eat & Drink

Our seasonal menu offers something for everyone, from hearty traditional cafe and pub-style meals to fresh vegetarian and vegan dishes. Start your day with a delicious breakfast, enjoy a relaxed lunch, or share scrumptious cakes and platters with friends.

To complete your visit, choose from our extensive drinks list including great coffee, craft beers, quality wines and cheeky cocktails. Dine in or out on the deck, or grab takeaway and a coffee for a stroll around the lake.

Functions

Business or pleasure, stand up or sit down, large or small, we can host your event in one of our private rooms or you can have the venue exclusively for your guests. Expect fabulous service, catering and beverage options, plus reliable, up-to-date A/V, sound equipment and disability access.

The Cafe

A bright, vibrant space with water views and a large central bar, perfect for larger groups with a variety of layout options. Ideal for large corporate events, functions, weddings, birthdays, celebrations and wakes.

  • Minimum spend: $4000
  • Capacity: 90 seated, 180 standing

Commodore's Room

Ideally suited to smaller functions and corporate meetings, this private room enjoys commanding views of Lake Wendouree, with its own bar facilities and access to an outdoor deck. The Commodore's Room is subject to availability due to Yacht Club club functions.

  • Minimum spend: $1500
  • Capacity: 40 seated, 50 standing

The Deck Bar

Newly refurbished to make the most of warm sunny days, the deck is Ballarat's most memorable and unique water's edge function space, with a fully serviced private bar.

Entire Venue

Exclusive use of the whole venue for large events, with full access to all bars, configured to best suit your needs. Ideal for functions, weddings, birthdays, celebrations and wakes.

  • Minimum spend: $5000
  • Capacity: 150 seated, 300 standing (400 including deck)

Capacities are maximums, not requirements to hire a space. Marquee hire is available for the deck area at an additional cost.

Thinking of something special?

If you need something not listed here, get in touch. Kelli, our Functions Manager, is happy to talk through your plans and create your perfect event. Call Kelli directly on 0423 691 217.

Function Menu

Our dedicated chefs pride themselves on the variety and quality of dishes for both cocktail-style events and sit-down dinner parties. Choose from our set function menus, or work with our Event Manager to tailor a menu for your group, catering for all budgets. A range of additional food boards is also available.

Gift Vouchers

Give the gift of a lakeside breakfast or lunch. Choose a value between $25 and $250, and we'll email the voucher straight to your recipient, or to you so you can print it out and include it with your gift.

Vouchers are not redeemable for cash and expire 36 months from the date of issue.
