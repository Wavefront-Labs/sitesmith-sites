Little Miss Ts is a local coffee shop at 162 Turpin Rd, Labrador, run by Tiahna and her team. Regulars come back for coffee that's always on point and for the genuinely kind, welcoming service, whatever sort of morning you're having.

Pull up a seat inside or out, or grab a takeaway on your walk. It's a cosy little setup, and there are usually a few happy pups around keen for a pat. One visitor called the blueberry cheesecake one of the best they'd had in a long time.
