Welcome To Copper Kitchen

Nestled in the heart of Noosa Heads, our authentic Indian restaurant Copper Kitchen has made its home in this beautiful coastal town. The food is fundamentally ingredient-driven and contemporary in creation.

With passion for perfection served on your platter, we deliver the most authentic experience of indulging in the spice of India's rich tangy, sweet, and spicy food culture. Every dish tells a story of tradition, crafted for the modern palate.

We offer a wide range of services including dine-in, takeaway, party food catering, and deliveries. We are fully licensed and BYO wine only.

Experience The Difference

Discover the joys of authentic Indian food. Explore our range of dishes from tandoori entrees to variety of naan breads, range of curries to rice delicacies.

All our food is prepared with original spices and is prepared fresh. Our chefs create meals with a magical mix of spices that elevate any dish to a whole new level.

Cooking Indian meals is an art, where our chefs artfully use spices that complement each particular dish to enhance its taste and create memorable flavors.

Our chefs use traditional clay oven known as tandoor for variety of dishes like tandoori chicken tikka and breads like naan to give them authentic and distinctive flavour.

Our menu is crafted with signature dishes by our experienced chef. Try our prawn Balti, lamb shanks, duck curry, and kadai gosht for a unique taste experience.

Our authentic Indian ginger chai is made to order by pounding fresh ginger in mortar and pestle, then steeping with Assam tea leaves, milk, and organic cane sugar.
