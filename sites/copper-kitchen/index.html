Nestled in the heart of Noosa Heads, Copper Kitchen is an authentic Indian restaurant that has made its home in this beautiful coastal town. Our food is fundamentally ingredient-driven and contemporary in creation, bringing you India's rich tangy, sweet and spicy food culture. Every dish tells a story of tradition, crafted for the modern palate.

We offer dine-in, takeaway, party food catering and deliveries, with tables inside and out. We are fully licensed and BYO wine only.

Experience the Difference

Discover the joys of authentic Indian food, from tandoori entrees and a variety of naan breads to a range of curries and rice dishes. Everything is prepared fresh with original spices, which our chefs use to complement each dish and create memorable flavours.

Our chefs cook in a traditional clay oven, the tandoor, for dishes like tandoori chicken tikka and breads like naan, giving them an authentic and distinctive flavour.

Chef's signature dishes

  • Prawn Balti
  • Lamb shanks
  • Duck curry
  • Kadai gosht

Ginger chai

Our authentic Indian ginger chai is made to order. We pound fresh ginger in a mortar and pestle, then steep it with Assam tea leaves, milk and organic cane sugar.
