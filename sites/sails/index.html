Sails Bar & Restaurant is a relaxed waterfront restaurant on the Cairns Esplanade, overlooking Trinity Inlet. Come in for breakfast by the water, settle in for a long lunch, or catch up over cocktails, wine and a cold Hemingway's beer.

The food is fresh, generous and made for the tropics. It brings together Australian produce, Asian fusion flavours and Peruvian influences in a menu designed for sharing, grazing or settling in for the full experience. No dress codes, just good food and easy vibes.

Breakfast by the Water

Sails serves breakfast Wednesday to Sunday from 8am to 11.30am, right on the Cairns Esplanade. Keep things classic or try something a little different. There's also a Little Sailors breakfast menu for kids 12 and under.

Coffee flows from opening, with juices, smoothies and mocktails too. From 10am, make a morning of it with a Mimosa, Bloody Mary, Espresso Martini or a Hemingway's breakfast beer.

Fresh Flavours Made for Cairns

The Sails menu moves easily between familiar favourites and flavours worth discovering. Share tacos, croquetas or crispy squid, tuck into beer-battered fish and chips or a Sails cheeseburger, or explore dishes inspired by the flavours of Asia and Peru.

Seafood plays a big part too, including our Sails Seafood Rice loaded with reef fish, prawns, scallop, squid, pipis and red claw yabby.

Drinks by the Water

A Cairns waterfront table deserves something cold beside it. Choose from Hemingway's beers, cocktails, wine, spirits and alcohol-free options, whether you're stopping in for a drink or settling down for a meal.

It's an easy spot for an afternoon cocktail, a beer with mates or a glass of wine while the day winds down over Trinity Inlet.

Family-Friendly Dining

Bringing the kids? They're very welcome. Sails is a casual, family-friendly restaurant with a dedicated Little Sailors menu and plenty of options for younger diners.

There's no need to save Sails for a special occasion. Come straight from exploring Cairns, bring the family, meet some friends and settle in by the water.

Bookings

Walk-ins are always welcome, but booking ahead is the best way to secure your table by the water. Reservations are highly recommended.

  • Table bookings are for a maximum of 25 people.
  • If you can't get your preferred date or time online, please call the venue and speak to one of our friendly staff about alternative times.
  • For a la carte bookings of 25 or more, email info_sails@hemingwaysbrewery.com or call +61 403 441 669.
  • For private parties and events, contact our event team at events@hemingwaysbrewery.com.
