After a beautiful morning walk on the beach or a refreshing dip in the ocean, there's nothing better than stopping by Chalk for a delicious Campos coffee or a revitalising meal. Dine in, grab a seat outside, or take it away.

At Chalk we believe you don't have to choose between eating healthy and indulging in treats. Our menu caters to all tastes, with vegetarian, vegan and gluten free options alongside the classics, so you can have the best of both worlds.
