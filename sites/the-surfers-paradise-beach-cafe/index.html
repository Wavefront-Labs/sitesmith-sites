The Surfers Paradise Beach Cafe is a modern cafe, pizzeria and bar on the Esplanade in the heart of Surfers Paradise, overlooking our famous beach and the beautiful Pacific Ocean. We serve breakfast, lunch and dinner in a breathtaking setting, and there's really nothing better than watching the waves while you eat.

We're family friendly with a full kids menu, so there's something for everyone. Daily specials are also available, and we're fully licensed with a selection of wines and beers. Check out our bar on Instagram at @surfersparadisebeach_bar.

What We Serve

Speciality pizzas, pasta, big breakfasts, pancakes, sandwiches, burgers, steaks. The choice is endless!

Breakfast

An extensive breakfast menu featuring our renowned Mediterranean Eggs Benedict, Belgian Waffles, Fresh Tropical Fruits, our infamous Big Brekkie and so much more.

Through the day

Freshly baked scones, a large variety of cakes and home-made muffins are available from 11am onwards throughout the day.

Lunch and dinner

Home-made pastas, stone-baked pizzas, grilled barramundi and steaks are just a few of our many lunch and dinner suggestions. Vegetarian options are available too.
