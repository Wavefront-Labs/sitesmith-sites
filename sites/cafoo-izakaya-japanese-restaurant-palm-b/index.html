Cafoo Izakaya serves Japanese tapas-style cuisine from Shop 6, Fifth Ave on the Gold Coast Highway at Palm Beach. Order a few plates to share, from crispy karaage and grilled scallops to char-grilled skewers, and pair them with sake, shochu cocktails or a glass of wine.

You can dine in or sit outside, grab takeaway, or book a table. Guests rate us 4.7 from 280 reviews and often mention the fresh sashimi, generous sharing plates and friendly staff who serve with a smile.
