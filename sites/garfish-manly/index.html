With Manly Wharf and the harbour as a backdrop, Garfish Manly offers the perfect blend of refined seafood dining and relaxed coastal charm, with a view that's hard to beat. We're a go-to seafood restaurant for lunch, dinner and special occasions, whether it's a leisurely seafood lunch, a romantic sunset dinner or a memorable corporate event in our private function room.

Our approach to the menu is simple. We work closely with trusted suppliers from Australia and New Zealand, with some international seafood also on the menu, and let premium ingredients shine as the heroes of each dish. Our award-winning chefs serve everything from perfectly cooked fish and oysters on ice to vibrant sides and coastal classics.

Our Menu

Our menu reflects the essence of seafood dining on the Northern Beaches: fresh, seasonal and full of flavour. Menus change based on seasonality and availability, and our blackboard specials change daily, inspired by the freshest seasonal produce and market availability.

Please let your waiter know of any allergies or specific dietary requirements. While all due care will be taken, if you are anaphylactic or have a severe allergy we are unable to absolutely guarantee cross contamination will not occur.

A 10% surcharge applies on weekends and 15% on public holidays.

Garfish Deck

Directly across from Manly Ferry Wharf, the Garfish Deck is a stylish cocktail bar for drinks by the water. With panoramic views across the cove and a covered waterfront deck, it's the spot for cocktails, light snacks and easy conversation, open from midday until late.

Whether you're stopping by for an afternoon spritz or starting your date night with a view, the Deck offers the perfect mix of seaside sophistication and laid-back charm.

Groups, Functions and Private Dining

Set against Manly Harbour, Garfish has spaces for everything from long lunches and birthday dinners to engagement parties, corporate events and cocktail-style functions of up to 100. Let us handle the details. You just enjoy the seafood, the company and the view.

Group Dining: up to 11 guests

Easily secure your table online for groups of up to 11 people.

Large Group Bookings: 12-16 guests

Customise a set menu and beverages to be enjoyed in the bright and bustling atmosphere of the restaurant or the quieter cosy corner of our lounge bar. Our semi-private spaces give your table a dedicated area with the atmosphere of the restaurant still around you. Perfect for casual get-togethers, hens parties and birthday celebrations.

Private Dining Room: up to 35 guests

Perfect for corporate dinners, engagement parties, baby showers, bridal showers and private celebrations, with attentive service, curated set menus and a considered beverage offering.

Exclusive Venue Hire: up to 100 guests

Full use of the restaurant for larger occasions like christenings and engagements, from arrival drinks through to a full sit-down dining experience.
