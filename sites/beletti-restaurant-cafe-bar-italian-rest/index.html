Step into Beletti, where great food meets genuine hospitality. Whether you're after your morning coffee, a chilled breakfast, a long lunch or dinner with family and friends, we serve fresh, modern Italian cuisine with a relaxed, local vibe. Our contemporary dining room, with its natural light, is a welcoming space for casual catch-ups, business lunches, special celebrations and everything in between.

Beletti is owned and operated by hospitality veterans Robert, with over 35 years of industry experience, and Vera, who has lived and breathed hospitality her entire life. We're proud to be part of Dandenong's story and here to make every visit feel like home. Come in once and you'll feel like part of the family.

Functions at Beletti

With over 30 years of experience in event management, service and food are our speciality. We cater for birthdays, engagements, christenings, Christmas parties, 18th and 21st birthday parties, weddings and all events.

We specialise in catering for the corporate sector and will design your function to meet your requirements. Beletti is the perfect venue for your next conference, meeting or function.

  • Breakfast meetings with barista style coffee
  • Lunch meetings with the option of an a la carte menu or 2 course set menu
  • An afternoon presentation with platters of homemade canapés served with a few drinks
  • Casual dinner meetings or a formal 2 or 3 course dinner

Our team is experienced in organising any type of event and we pride ourselves on attention to every detail. Audio visual equipment is available on request.

For function enquiries, email admin@beletti.com.au or call Vera on 0438 011 725 or 9793 1600.

Weddings

Congratulations on your engagement! We know your wedding is one of the most important days of your life. Whether you're planning an intimate gathering or a large celebration, Beletti offers everything you need to make it a memorable one.

Robert and Vera Moretti are experienced in organising any type of event, and every wedding at Beletti is designed to suit the bride and groom's individual needs.

Our wedding packages include

  • Complimentary canapés on arrival
  • White linen tablecloths and napkins
  • Main table and cake table linen

Entertainment and chair covers are available on request.

Spaces

  • Private room: an intimate yet sophisticated setting for weddings of 40 to 80 guests seated
  • Exclusive use of the restaurant: weddings of 80 to 120 guests seated, or 100 to 160 guests in a cocktail set up

Catering

Beletti offers catering to suit any type of event and budget, with menus tailored to your requirements. Catering can be picked up from the restaurant or delivered straight to your office, home or venue. Grazing tables are also available.

Please speak to Vera to discuss your options.
