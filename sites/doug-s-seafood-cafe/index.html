Doug's Seafood Cafe is a family business on Flinders Parade in Sandgate, serving breakfast, lunch and dinner seven days a week. Grab a seat on the terrace overlooking the beach and enjoy burgers, salads and the catch of the day in an easygoing setting.

We're pet friendly, and the dog friendly beach is just across the road, so bring the dog along. Regulars come back for our barramundi, snapper, cod and red emperor, our prawn cutlets, our grilled octopus and a generous breakfast with good coffee. Eat in, take away or get it delivered.
