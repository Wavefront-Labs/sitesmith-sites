Blaktan Coffee Kitchen is a coffee roasting cafe serving modern Asian cuisine, tucked away at 6/4-8 Burke Cres, North Lakes. It's a mellow, modern space where Asian fusion food sits alongside our coffee and cocktails.

Guests tell us they come for the coffee and stay for the atmosphere. Some have lingered more than two hours. Our young, friendly team takes care to serve generous plates that look as good as they taste, like the much-loved Bulgogi Eggs Bene and our mushrooms at breakfast. If you like your coffee a little lighter, just ask and we'll happily adjust it.
