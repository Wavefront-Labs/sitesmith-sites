Gimme One Fried Skewers (来一串儿) is the epitome of Chinese street food culture. You build your own selection from a diverse and quirky menu, at affordable prices, and our customers keep coming back for more.

Our Hurstville store is at Shop 3A/225H Forest Rd, by Hurstville Station. Grab a handful of skewers for a quick, flavourful snack, either takeaway or delivered, with vegetarian options too.

How It Works

Pick the skewers you like, choose how much heat you want, and we fry them up fresh. If you love real chilli, go for extra spicy. Our regulars say the rice cake is a must.

  • Build your own selection
  • Takeaway and delivery
  • Vegetarian options
  • Our seasoning is available to buy in store, so you can take the flavour home
