Located within The Wodonga Country Club in picturesque North East Victoria, The Clubhouse Bar & Bistro is a spectacular venue suited for all occasions. The fully licensed Bistro, Lounge and Spike Bar caters to members, visitors and the public alike.

With views over the golf course and private surrounds, it's an easy escape for a catch up with friends, a casual family dinner or a special celebration. Bookings are welcome, and takeaway is available.

The Bistro

Be surprised! Our menu offers a wide selection, from the grill to salads or a seafood option, finishing off with a tasty sweet dessert. Our aim is appetising and delicious value-for-money meals in a relaxing atmosphere. Come, experience and enjoy.

Quick lunches

Ask about our $10 summer Express lunch options. Short on time? Email through a lunch pre-order form and your meal will be ready to enjoy.

The Spike Bar

Whether it's social, business, a game of pool or darts, a flutter at the TAB or just a catch up with friends, the Spike Bar welcomes everyone. Enjoy a drink overlooking the picturesque 9th green. You don't need to be a member of the golf club to enjoy our facility.

Holding over 200 guests, the Spike Bar is also a great venue for more casual functions, parties and special occasions.

Open daily

  • Monday: 12.00am to 4.00pm
  • Tuesday: 12.30pm to 3.00pm
  • Wednesday to Sunday: 11am to late

Functions at the Clubhouse

Formal or casual, corporate or private, large or small, we have options for any special event. Our two newly renovated function rooms cater for groups of 30 to 200, perfect for weddings, birthdays, family events, corporate golf days and conferences.

Our experienced kitchen has developed event menus suited to most occasions, with sit-down menus or fancy finger food. Have a special request? Our team will happily work with you to put together a package to suit.

  • Platters
  • Standard alternate serve menu
  • Premium alternate serve menu
  • Gourmet canapes
