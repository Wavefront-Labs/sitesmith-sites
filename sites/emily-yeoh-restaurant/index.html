At Emily Yeoh, we promise to treat you with respect and kindness, serve delicious food and exceptional wine, and welcome you as a friend and family. Emily offers her Asian-fusion-inspired dishes in a casual and relaxed dining atmosphere in the beautiful suburb of Paddington, Brisbane.

Specialties include Roast Duck, Char-Xiu Pork, and street food from Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, South Korea, Japan and Hong Kong.

Lunch and Bookings

Emily Yeoh now offers Thursday to Sunday lunchtime takeaway. Order your lunch via Uber Eats or call us directly for pick-up.

Please note that weekday lunchtime service is takeaway only, with no dine-in. Dine-in lunch is still available on Saturdays and Sundays as usual.

  • Hours: 11 AM to 2:30 PM and 5 PM to 8:45 PM
  • For bookings over 6 people, please call or email
  • Big bookings require a minimum of 24 hours' notice

Our Famous Mud Crabs

New menus are now in place, and we've kept the crowd favourites you know and love. Our famous Singaporean Chilli and Black Pepper Mud Crabs are here to stay, and they're more popular than ever. We also have two new flavours: Ginger Shallot and Creamy Garlic Butter.

Crabs are now available shelled or de-shelled for your convenience. These signature dishes are known to sell out fast, so we highly recommend pre-ordering to avoid missing out.

Reserve your crab

To enjoy our de-shelled Mud Crabs, please pre-order by:

  • Emailing us at reservations@emilyyeoh.com.au
  • Including your request in the notes section of your booking

Please note that 24 to 48 hours' notice is required. Same-day requests cannot always be accommodated. Thank you for your understanding.

Gift Cards and House-Made Sauces

Grab a gift card for that impossible-to-buy-for special someone, or pick up one of our house-made sauces and become a MasterChef yourself. Sauces are made fresh in-house, so please give us a call and pick up at the restaurant.

  • EY Spicy Sambal Sauce: perfect for nasi lemak, noodles, laksa, and even on your poached egg, avo and toast. Ask Emily for her secret hangover breakfast recipe with sambal sauce.
  • EY Peking Duck Sauce: designed specially for Peking duck, and also good with roast pork or even roast lamb.
  • A classic EY sambal sauce with a twist: caramelised pineapple and kaffir lime leaves with a hint of cinnamon and clove. It's perfect for roast pork or even steak. Try it in a Bloody Mary. Emily calls it a Bloody Emily.
  • EY Laksa Paste: Emily's signature laksa paste, which comes with her secret recipe. Made with mostly fresh ingredients from her own garden, it's a multi-purpose kitchen favourite. Try laksa fried rice, a laksa omelette or your own curry laksa at home.
