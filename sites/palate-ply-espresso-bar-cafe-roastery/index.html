Palate & Ply is a funky, friendly cafe and roastery at 37 Vernon Street, Coffs Harbour, family owned and run by Brent and Susan Roulstone. We run on friendliness, laughter and lots of cups of awesome coffee, with fresh roasted beans, wholesome food and a relaxed vibe away from the bustle.

We're your home away from home, a place to relax and catch up with family and friends. There's plenty of space inside and out for the whole family, or grab a takeaway on the fly.

The Cafe

We're open 6 days a week for dine in or takeaway. Our doors open at 6:30am every morning, ready to welcome you in with the smell of freshly roasted coffee.

Our philosophy is simple: a safe, clean and hygienic venue with happy staff, happy customers, great coffee and delicious hearty meals. Whether you're catching up with friends and family, holding a business meeting at a larger table, fresh from a workout or just after a spot to chill in one of our private lounges or outside in the fresh air, we have the right spot for you.

Coffee and beverages

Our fresh artisan coffee is hand roasted by Big 5 Coffee Roasters using only the best Arabica beans. Something a little niche is our own cold brew Nitro Coffee, and we also pour a selection of fine teas, turmeric, chai, smoothies, milkshakes and more.

Breakfasts, lunches and light snacks

Get ready for a Classic Breakfast, fritters to die for, eggs Bennie (a favourite) and our house made speciality granola. For lunch, there's everything from toasties to our popular corn fritters. Using fresh and sustainable local produce, everything on the menu is full of flavour and prepared on site, so you enjoy it at its best.

Office, Meeting & Event Catering

Need catering for your function, event or business meeting? We offer our full breakfast and lunch menu options as well as seasonal platters and sandwiches, with options both in-house and delivered.

Meetings & Co-working

Work in comfort with free fast Wi-Fi, plenty of power outlets, air-conditioning and of course awesome food and coffee. We have a large space with large tables and plenty of quiet spots to work.

Book a table or lounge area for your next meeting, interview or catch up, or host a meeting or event in our in-house space. Various meeting packages are available. Get in touch for details and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Big 5 Coffee Roasters

Big 5 Coffee Roasters have perfected their blends for milk based coffees. Throughout the year we also offer single origin coffees that showcase a particular region, variety or even a particular grower. They're carefully roasted on our small batch roaster and are exceptional for black espresso and a range of brew methods.

Roasting takes place weekly so our coffee is at its best when served. Our beans are all Arabica, sourced from sustainable regions around the world, and the roasting process includes an additional cleaning step for a clean, fresh tasting cup.
