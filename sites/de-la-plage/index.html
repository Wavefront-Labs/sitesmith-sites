De la Plage is a relaxed beach cafe at the Surf Life Saving Club in Brinkin, serving fresh food and delicious coffee with a sublime seaside location and a friendly, laid-back atmosphere.

We source quality produce and ingredients from local farmers, aiming to deliver the best fresh and organic food. Everything is prepared fresh on the premises, and you'll find a good variety of vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options on the menu.

Our Food

Fresh and healthy food at a great price. All our food is prepared fresh on the premises and we try our very best to use local and organic ingredients.

Our ever-changing menu is inspired by Darwin's culturally diverse community and fresh, organic produce. Gluten-free and vegan options are available.

What's On

Our events focus on embracing different cultures through food and supporting local artists. We also offer fun and affordable workshops. Check our Facebook Event Calendar for the latest events.

  • Fish & Chips: local wild caught Barra, every Friday night from 5pm.
  • Sunday Sessions: De la Plage and The Surf Club provide a free event for the community, supporting local artists and musicians, every Sunday evening.
  • Street Food: embracing different cultures from around the world, on the first Sunday of every month from 6pm.

The club's licensed bar is open on Fridays and Sundays from 5pm until 8pm.

Catering

Inspiring flavours for any occasion. We specialise in birthdays, business lunches and more.

Our Story

Claire Pirau and Sevan Guzel met in 2013 while working at cafes and restaurants in the Darwin CBD. Their aligned philosophies on food, business and life led to the birth of De la Plage, which means 'from the beach' in French, in 2014.

Their vision was to bring different cultures together through food. The cafe is built on cultural diversity, with a menu inspired by Claire's Belgian background and Sevan's Turkish roots, the multiculturalism within the team, and the broader Darwin community.

Since then, De la Plage has become much more than a cafe. With live music, community events and workshops held throughout the year, it's a fun and relaxed space for people from all walks of life.
