Phở Hương is a Hanoi-style phở shop on Chapel Road in Bankstown, serving beef and chicken phở in clear, flavourful broths. Bowls come generously topped with tender sliced beef or chicken, plenty of green shallots, crisp bean sprouts and fresh herbs.

Drop in to dine in, grab takeaway, order delivery or make a booking. Our customers rate us 4.7 from 235 Google reviews, and we appreciate everyone who takes the time to share their experience.
