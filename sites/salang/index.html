Salang is a traditional Afghan restaurant run by husband and wife Farid and Narges Bahrami, who between them have more than 30 years' experience serving the very best of Afghan cuisine for any occasion. They opened Salang from scratch in 2010, when interest in Afghan food was growing fast but there was no upbeat, modern venue for fine dining.

Every dish is prepared in-house as you order, using the freshest top-quality ingredients. Our chef cooks each meal just the way she would for her own family at home, and the menu is wide enough for every kind of food lover. We want every guest to leave happy and keep coming back for years to come.

Functions and Catering

Our modern restaurant comfortably seats up to 100 people, making it a great spot for birthdays, work parties and get-togethers.

If your event is too big for the restaurant, we also cater for special occasions such as weddings, engagements and anniversaries, bringing the same great service and cuisine to your celebration.
