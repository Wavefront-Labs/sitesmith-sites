Right in the heart of Penrith CBD on Belmore Street, The T-Spoon Cafe Penrith opened in 2023 and is the highest-rated location in the T-Spoon network, with a 4.8 star average on Google. Sitting at street level among offices, shops and services, it's an easy stop for a morning coffee before work, a mid-morning brunch meeting or a relaxed lunch with friends.

Expect a warm, contemporary space, fast and friendly service, and the full T-Spoon menu made fresh every day. Dine in, sit outside, grab takeaway or have it delivered. We're part of The T-Spoon Cafe family, an independent Sydney cafe group built since 2019 on fresh food, genuine hospitality and a real connection to the communities we serve.
