Thai House Helensvale is a Thai restaurant at Shop 39/12 Sir John Overall Dr, Helensvale. Come in and dine with us inside or at an outdoor table, grab takeaway, or have it delivered. Vegetarian options are on the menu and bookings are welcome.

We're rated 4.6 from 298 Google reviews. Guests have shared kind words about the tender, juicy grilled pork skewers, the bold Tom Yum and a friendly chat with the owner.
