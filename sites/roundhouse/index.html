Roundhouse sits on Level 9 at 282 King St, at the top of Crystalbrook Kingsley in Newcastle's old council building. Guests come for the sweeping rooftop views over the city and the harbour, and for service they describe as relaxed and professional.

The menu leans on local producers, from Pukara Estate olives and oil to Ulladulla bluefin tuna and Rangers Valley beef. Bookings can be made online through SevenRooms at sevenrooms.com/reservations/roundhousenewcastle.
