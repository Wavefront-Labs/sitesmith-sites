Breakfast

A Breakfast Worth Waking Up For | $39 per person

They say breakfast is the most important meal of the day - we couldn’t agree more.

Served seven days a week, our buffet breakfast brings together generous hot selections, beautifully presented continental favourites and a dedicated egg station, all designed for lingering mornings and second plates.

Build your perfect breakfast from made to order eggs, crisp bacon, sausages and seasonal hot dishes, or keep things light with a colourful spread of fresh fruit, yoghurt and cereals. Add artisan bakery items, toasted just the way you like them, alongside cold meats, cheeses and classic accompaniments.

What’s on the buffet - Made to order egg station - Full hot breakfast selection - Fresh fruit, yoghurt and cereal bar - Cold meats and cheeses - Artisan bakery items and toast - Coffee, tea and juices (Barista coffee available at an additional charge)

Crystalbrook Conferences and Events

Your next big thing, sorted

From boardroom brainstorms to weddings with wow-factor, our spaces were made for big moments. Whether you're planning a power meeting, cocktail soirée, team getaway or something totally personal, we’ve got the venue (and the vibe) to match. Across our properties, you’ll find modern event spaces with natural light, top-notch tech, and menus worth RSVPing for. And because we like to keep things easy, everything you need is right here; accommodation, dining, a drink (or two) after.

Conference & Event Spaces

Cairns

Looking to host something special in Cairns? Crystalbrook Riley, Crystalbrook Flynn and Crystalbrook Bailey bring modern event spaces, rooftop bars, and relaxed tropical vibes together in the heart of the city.

From high-impact conferences to easygoing gatherings, our venues cater for up to 330 guests, and with nine restaurants and bars between the three hotels, there’s plenty of room to mix business with pleasure. Think post-meeting cocktails, poolside downtime or a little spa indulgence (you’ve earned it).

Brisbane

Set beneath Brisbane’s Story Bridge, Crystalbrook Vincent flips the script on traditional events. With three flexible spaces for up to 150 guests, it’s all about big ideas, bold art and even bolder views.

Add in rooftop cocktails, riverside dining and an atmosphere that’s more energising than your morning espresso, this is business, reimagined.

Byron Bay

Nestled in 45 acres of subtropical rainforest, Crystalbrook Byron is where work and wellness go hand in hand. From intimate strategy sessions to events for up to 150 guests, it’s a place to gather, focus and breathe easy.

Think serene surrounds, sustainably-minded dining, daily yoga, a heated infinity pool and spaces that feel more retreat than boardroom. Productivity looks a little different here... and that’s kind of the point.

Newcastle

In the heart of Newcastle, Crystalbrook Kingsley brings together heritage charm, modern sophistication and a rooftop view worth planning for. Whether it’s a boardroom meeting or a skyline celebration, our flexible spaces cater for up to 150 guests.

With two dedicated meeting rooms, sustainably-led dining and a strong sense of local culture, this is where connection and creativity come naturally, plus, the rooftop bar doesn’t hurt.

Weddings

You’ve pinned the dresses, bookmarked the florals and debated doughnuts vs cake (why not both?). Now comes the real “yes,” the venue.

Whether it’s rainforest romance in Byron Bay, rooftop views in Newcastle or tropical glam in Cairns, Crystalbrook weddings are anything but ordinary. Think stylish spaces, local flavour, and a team that actually gets your vision, from the big wow to the tiny details.

Modern, memorable, and more than a little photogenic. Just how you imagined it.
