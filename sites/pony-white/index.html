Pony White is a relaxed, cosy cafe at 379 George St, Fitzroy, with lovely decor and a menu that stands apart from the usual Melbourne brunch. Think French toast de matcha and an okonomiyaki Benedict, plus plenty of vegetarian options.

There's room to roam, so it suits a brunch with little ones, and four-legged friends are welcome too. Dine in, sit outside, book ahead, or grab it as takeaway or delivery.
