Dust Temple is your Currumbin Waters cafe by day, serving exceptional coffee and curated drinks in our beautifully refreshed space. Under new ownership, we've transformed this Currumbin gem into the Gold Coast's go-to spot for daytime caffeine and evening creativity.

We're open Tuesday to Sunday, 7am to 1:30pm, and events run late. From morning coffee runs to midnight poetry, Dust Temple is where Currumbin's creative community connects.
