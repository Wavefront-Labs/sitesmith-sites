About Dust

Dust Temple: Currumbin’s Creative Haven

Dust Temple is your Currumbin Waters cafe destination by day – serving exceptional coffee and curated drinks in our beautifully refreshed space. Under new ownership, we’ve transformed this Currumbin gem into the Gold Coast’s go-to spot for daytime caffeine and evening creativity.

Locally-roasted specialty coffee

Curated tea, cold drinks & refreshments

Perfect workspace or casual meetup spot

Live music events with house DJs

Poetry nights & intimate performances

Fully licensed bar (craft beers, wines, cocktails)

Private Events: Host your next gathering – corporate functions, birthdays, creative workshops – for 20-200 guests in our versatile renovated space.

Open Tues-Sun 7am-1:30pm (events extend late). From morning coffee runs to midnight poetry, Dust Temple is where Currumbin’s creative community connects.
