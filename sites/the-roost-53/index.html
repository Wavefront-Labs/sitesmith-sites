The Roost @ 53 is a concept cafe in the Gold Coast suburb of Southport. Whether you're on the go or would like a quiet afternoon in our shaded dining area, The Roost offers an escape from the everyday (as well as first class grub and great coffee).

We're a cosy space where plenty of regulars are greeted by name, and we'd love to see you soon.
