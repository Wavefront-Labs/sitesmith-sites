About

Sum Sum brings Vietnamese cooking to Melbourne Street, South Brisbane, with premium ingredients, rich flavours and warm hospitality. We honour tradition with a modern touch, in a welcoming space made for a genuine night out.

Our menu is designed by Chef Aaron and draws on his French culinary background, Michelin-starred experience in Japan and travels across Asia. Each dish pairs authentic Vietnamese flavours with modern techniques. Join us for dinner Wednesday to Saturday, from 17:00 until late, for dine-in or takeaway, with vegetarian options available.
