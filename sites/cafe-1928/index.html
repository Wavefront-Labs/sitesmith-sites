Cafe 1928 sits in the Bundaberg Botanic Gardens on the city's north side, right alongside the Hinkler Hall of Aviation and the other attractions in Bundaberg's favourite tropical outdoor precinct. Our friendly team prides itself on great coffee, fresh light food options and a relaxed, cosmopolitan atmosphere.

Drop in for one of our famous coffees after a wander through the gardens, or get in touch to book your next event. We offer dine in, takeaway and outdoor seating.

Functions and Weddings

Cafe 1928 is a BYO venue that operates until 11.45pm, so you can host a wide range of events and functions in the style you prefer. Whether it's a wedding with scenic garden backdrops or a special place to unwind with friends and family, we can cater for your needs.
