Cafe Minnie is a small cafe tucked away at 1c/72 Minnie St, Southport. Regulars call it a hidden gem, with a peaceful, relaxed atmosphere, friendly staff and owners who take real pride in what they do.

Guests rave about fresh, tasty food, generous portions and great drinks, and we're proud to hold a 5-star rating from 69 reviews. You can dine in, grab takeaway, order delivery or make a booking, and there are vegetarian options too.
