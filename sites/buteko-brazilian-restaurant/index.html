Buteko is traditionally known as a chill-out venue where great drinks and food are served: a place to share good vibes, laughs and a chat without obligation. We're family friendly, and yes, that includes your four-legged members too.

As the first and only Brazilian restaurant in Scarborough, we're the place to be all year long, with free live music and entertainment, daily specials, good meals, drinks and specialty cocktails. Come and say hi, meet our friendly team and share the Buteko experience.
