The Waterfront Restaurant sits on the beach in Dayman Park, Urangan, with the most enviable view in Hervey Bay. We're open for breakfast and lunch Wednesday to Sunday, 8am to 3pm, serving fresh seafood, steaks and generous plates looking out over the water.

We're a family owned and operated business, built in 2004. We buy fresh local produce and seafood whenever we can, and we bake all of our desserts and cakes on the premises.

Weddings

Looking for a beautiful waterfront location? You won't get closer than this. At high tide the restaurant sits in the ocean, and at low tide it sits on the beach, with uninterrupted views of the calm waters of Hervey Bay and World Heritage-listed Fraser Island.

Waterfront weddings are affordable for all and start from $1200.00 for our all-exclusive Simply Elope Package. We're just 300 km north of Brisbane, with daily direct flights from Sydney, which makes us a popular choice for destination weddings.

Our in-house wedding planner, Natalie Stone, was 2014 Queensland's Number One Wedding Planner and has been named the nation's best in-house wedding planner by the Australian Bridal Industry Awards. She has a decade of experience and hundreds of weddings behind her. Natalie has created the ultimate dream wedding package with some of the region's finest suppliers. She can also help you tailor your own package to suit your style, taste and budget.

Talk to Natalie on 0402550558 or email waterfrontherveybay@gmail.com.

A huge thank you to our past brides and grooms for voting for us. Our aim is to help our couples plan their special day and make it one of the most memorable days of their lives.

A Local Family Business

The Wolff family bought the local tourist attraction Reefworld Aquarium in 1989. After years of running a small cafe, they branched out and built the Waterfront Restaurant in 2004.

With no real experience in owning a restaurant, the family threw themselves in head first and worked hard to earn one of the highest reputations in the Australian bridal industry. The business takes pride in supporting the local community through employment and work experience, and by buying fresh local produce and seafood when possible.

Jan and Ross Wolff are now retired, but you'll still see them working or dining at the restaurant. Their children Greg and Natalie, and daughter-in-law Natasha, still work in the family business. Come and join us for the breathtaking views and delicious food.
