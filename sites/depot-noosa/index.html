A PLACE MADE FOR NOOSA

Good Food. Easy Days.

Take a seat overlooking the beautiful Noosa River and enjoy exceptional food, really good coffee and relaxed coastal hospitality at Depot Noosa. Serving all day brunch alongside freshly brewed Nash Lane Coffee, we’re a place where locals and visitors alike come to slow down, reconnect and experience everything that makes Noosaville and Gympie Terrace so special.

We believe great cafés are about more than just what’s on the plate—they’re about bringing people together and creating memorable moments. Hospitality is our vessel for human connection, whether you’re stopping by for your morning flat white, a long brunch with friends, a leisurely lunch or an afternoon drink by the river.

Depot Noosa Frequently Asked Questions

Visiting Depot Noosa

The Depot Noosa Menu

Does Depot Noosa serve breakfast all day?

Yes, Depot Noosa offers a range of fresh breakfast and brunch favourites designed to suit both locals and visitors looking for quality café dining in Noosaville.

What type of food can I find on the Depot Noosa menu?

The Depot Noosa menu features fresh breakfast dishes, brunch favourites, lunch options, café classics, smoothies, drinks and specialty coffee designed around relaxed Sunshine Coast dining.

Does Depot Noosa cater for different dietary preferences?

Depot Noosa offers a variety of menu options and seasonal dishes that may suit different dietary requirements. Speak with our friendly team in store for specific menu information and availability.

Where Depot Noosa Café is located

Where is Depot Noosa located?

Depot Noosa is located on iconic Gympie Terrace in Noosaville, adjacent to the beautiful Noosa River and within easy walking distance of local accommodation, parks and riverside attractions.

Is Depot Noosa close to the Noosa River?

Yes, Depot Noosa is positioned just moments from the Noosa River, making it an ideal place to stop for breakfast, coffee or lunch while enjoying the relaxed Noosaville atmosphere.

Is parking available near Depot Noosa?

Yes, there is public parking available throughout Gympie Terrace and surrounding areas, making Depot Noosa easily accessible for both locals and visitors.

The Depot Noosa Staff & Café Culture

What coffee does Depot Noosa serve?

Depot Noosa proudly serves specialty coffee from Nash Lane, known for quality roasting, rich flavour profiles and consistently great coffee experiences.

Can I purchase Nash Lane coffee beans from Depot Noosa?

Yes, freshly roasted Nash Lane coffee beans are available to purchase in store so you can enjoy your favourite coffee blend at home.

What makes Depot Noosa different from other cafés in Noosa?

Depot Noosa combines quality food, specialty coffee, friendly staff and a relaxed Sunshine Coast atmosphere to create a welcoming space for both locals and visitors.
