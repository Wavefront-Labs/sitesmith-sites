Found in Noosaville on the edge of the Noosa River, Depot serves all-day brunch and specialty coffee by Nash Lane. It's a favourite spot for locals and visitors to enjoy the best of Noosaville, 7 days a week.

Take a seat overlooking the river, indoors or out on the patio, and enjoy good food, really good coffee and relaxed coastal hospitality. We're open 7 days, 5.30am to 3pm, on Gympie Terrace.

Good Food. Easy Days.

We believe great cafés are about more than what's on the plate. They're about bringing people together and creating memorable moments. Hospitality is our vessel for human connection, whether you're stopping by for your morning flat white, a long brunch with friends, a leisurely lunch or an afternoon drink by the river.

  • Exceptional coffee: every cup brewed with care using freshly roasted Nash Lane beans.
  • Fresh food: seasonal ingredients, generous portions and all-day brunch made to savour.
  • Slow mornings: take your time, soak up the river views and enjoy the relaxed pace of Noosa.
  • Real hospitality: friendly service, genuine smiles and a place where everyone feels welcome.

Good to Know

Brunch all day

Our menu covers breakfast and brunch favourites, lunch options, café classics, smoothies, drinks and specialty coffee, served all day.

Dietary needs

We have a range of menu options and seasonal dishes that may suit different dietary requirements, including vegetarian choices. Have a chat with our team in store about specific dishes and availability.

Nash Lane beans to take home

Freshly roasted Nash Lane coffee beans are available to buy in store, so you can enjoy your favourite blend at home.

By the river

We're on Gympie Terrace, moments from the Noosa River and within easy walking distance of local accommodation, parks and riverside attractions. Bookings are welcome on 0417 835 371 or bookings@depotnoosa.com.
