Set on Honeysuckle Drive with views of the Hunter River, Monte is for laidback brekkies, catch-ups with friends, takeaway goodies, and days that you never want to end. We serve an all-day brunch menu, baked treats and damn good coffee.

Dine in, grab something to take away, or find a spot at one of our outdoor tables near the water. Bookings are welcome, and you can reserve a table online.
