Manly Deck Bar & Restaurant is located at the heart of Manly Village. With a deck overlooking the beautiful Manly Harbour, it is the ideal place to put your feet up and relax. Enjoy a great selection of tap beers, wines and other spirits, alongside great food.

Manly Deck Bar & Restaurant is Manly Harbour’s Funky, Modern & Relaxing Bar. Oozing with a Cruisey Atmosphere, Quality Food & Staff, the best part about it is the Prime Location & Views of the Marina, Ocean and Moreton Island.. What more could you want in a Local ??

In order to make healthy, responsible decisions about what we eat, we must know where our food comes from? We are proud to say that we support our Local farming community and “choose Local”. We make sure that every ingredient that goes into our dishes is fresh. One more addition to the fun atmosphere at Manly Deck is the Live Music happening throughout the Week. We have very talented musicians in and around Manly. It’s a Musical Heaven at the Deck every Tuesday to Sunday!

Manly Deck offers a variety of Food and Drinks throughout the week. Our customers can enjoy tasty and yummilicious meals at discounted prices.

A fishing village of the past, now a beautiful boat harbour with a wide variety of outdoor and cultural experiences to be had; that is Manly Village for you! Sailing, Bird-watching, Cycling, Swimming, Backpacking – great outdoor activities alongside a wide range of food and shopping options. We also host gatherings of upto 150 people at the Manly Deck Bar & Restaurant. So if you are already on the lookout for a location for your Christmas party, do let us know.

If you are planning on having a function, look no further than the Deck in our restaurant! The Deck area can be booked exclusively for a birthday party, an anniversary party, office party or any other occasion you want to celebrate. We have two slots for the deck booking everyday – lunch and dinner. You would need to be a minimum of 30 people if your booking is between Monday to Thursday and a minimum of 40 people if for Friday, Saturday or Sunday.

Please note the following information about function bookings.

  • Your booking fee will include use of the running tab and service of the Bar/Wait Staff
  • You can choose Hot & Cold Platters for your function
  • You can also choose to go for a 2-course or 3-course meal
  • You can also decide to go for a Buffet
  • You can run a Bar Tab at the function
  • You can bring Cake & Decorations* *There is a cleaning fee at the Management’s discretion, if decorations & rubbish have been left.
  • The booking is secured upon payment of fee
