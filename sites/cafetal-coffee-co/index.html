Castillo Fermented: Our Signature Coffee

Some coffees define a café. This is ours. The Castillo Fermented has been the beating heart of Cafetal Coffee Co. since we opened our doors in 2020. We've served it every single day to every kind of coffee lover, and it has never let us down.

It comes directly from Liliana Caballero at Hacienda Casablanca in the Santander highlands of Colombia, grown at 1,550 metres above sea level. It's the same farm, the same farmer and the same commitment to quality, year after year. A controlled, two-stage fermentation and washed process builds depth and complexity while keeping the bean's natural clarity.

Expect caramel sweetness, smooth honey and a clean citrus fruit finish. It pulls a beautiful espresso, is exceptional in milk-based drinks and holds its own as a long black. We think it'll become your coffee too.

  • Variety: Castillo
  • Process: 2-phase fermentation & washed
  • Roasting: Medium-Dark
  • Best for: Espresso, Milk-based coffee & Long Black
  • Flavour Profile: Caramel, Honey & Citrus Fruits
  • Producer: Liliana Caballero / Farm: Hacienda Casablanca
  • Elevation: 1550 meters
  • Region: Santander
  • Country: Colombia
  • Harvest Season: October–January

Decaf Apia

Decaf has never tasted like this. The Decaf Apia proves that removing caffeine doesn't have to mean removing character.

It's sourced directly from a group of smallholder farmers in the Apia region of Risaralda, Colombia, grown between 1,650 and 1,900 metres above sea level, and processed in partnership with Descafecol, Colombia's pioneering decaffeination specialists. Their Sugar Cane process uses ethyl acetate derived from fermented sugar cane to gently remove caffeine while leaving the bean's full flavour intact.

The result is citrus acidity, smooth caramel sweetness and a distinctive sugar cane finish. It's perfect for espresso, milk-based coffees and long blacks, any time of day.

  • Variety: Castillo
  • Process: Sugar Cane Natural Decaffeination Process
  • Roasting: Medium-Dark
  • Best for: Espresso, Milk-based coffee & Long Black
  • Flavour Profile: Citrus, Caramel & Sugar Cane
  • Producer: Group of smallholders from Apia & Descafecol
  • Elevation: 1650 to 1900 meters
  • Region: Apia - Risaralda
  • Country: Colombia
  • Harvest Season: October–January

Low Caffeine

All the flavour, half the caffeine. This is for people who love the ritual but want more control over their intake.

It's a 50/50 blend of Caturra Washed and Decaf Cordillera, naturally decaffeinated via the Sugar Cane process. It's sourced directly from Jairo Arcila in Colombia and grown between 1,450 and 1,700 metres above sea level. Expect orange citrus, smooth caramel sweetness and a sugar cane finish. It's great for espresso, milk-based coffees and long blacks, without the jitters.

  • Caffeine Level: 50% less Caffeine
  • Blend: 50% Decaf Cordillera + 50% Caturra Washed
  • Variety: Caturra & Castillo
  • Process: Fermented & Washed / Sugar Cane Natural Decaffeination Process
  • Roasting: Medium-Dark
  • Best for: Espresso, Milk-based coffee & Long Black
  • Flavour Profile: Orange, Caramel & Sugar Cane
  • Producer: Jairo Arcila & Descafecol
  • Elevation: 1450 to 1700 meters
  • Region: Quindio - Tolima
  • Country: Colombia
  • Harvest Season: October–January
