The Turret is a vibrant cafe in a beautifully restored 1890s heritage building on the corner of Sturt Street, Ballarat. Using fresh local produce, we offer a diverse modern menu with an extensive all-day breakfast and lunch, so you'll be spoiled for choice.

Everything is fresh, local and house made, finished to the highest standard by our team. Our team prides itself on delicious food, great coffee and excellent service. Dine in, sit outside, grab takeaway or book a table online.

Catering & Events

From preparing premium chicken breast in our kitchen to finishing every sauce by hand, all of our catering is made in-house by our experienced team. Every cake, slice and sweet treat is house-baked using our award-winning recipes.

We cater for corporate events, office lunches, boardroom meetings, training days, birthdays, private parties and special events. We can accommodate most tastes, including gluten free and vegetarian.

Pick up or delivery

Collect from us or have your order delivered to your office, venue or home across Ballarat and surrounding suburbs including Alfredton, Lucas, Wendouree, Delacombe and Sebastopol. Delivery outside Ballarat CBD (10km) is $35.

Catering orders are only accepted via our online ordering link. Select your menu, choose pick up or delivery and secure your date. For enquiries, email catering@turretcafe.com.au.
