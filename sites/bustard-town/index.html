Bustard Town is Darwin's award-winning live music venue: 1000sqm in the heart of the city, with a tropical alfresco bar and garden, a retro café and a renowned Bandroom set in a refurbished wartime hut.

We're independently owned and locally operated by industry veterans who love music. Come for a coffee and a French pastry in the morning, stay for wood-fired food, icy cocktails and a band at night.

Menu

Cafe

Step into our cosy little retro café or sink into a shady seat in our troppo courtyard. It's the perfect spot to chill, chat, or catch up on some reading.

We're serving Melbourne's finest Genovese coffee, alongside Darwin's best in-house baked French patisserie.

BT Alfresco

Bustard Town serves up relaxed alfresco dining in the heart of Darwin, surrounded by lush greenery and tropical breezes.

Our chefs fire up the wood oven and grill, serving smoky Mediterranean flavours, bold spices and unique dishes you won't find anywhere else, from wood-fired pizzas to open-flame Argentinean barbecue.

With icy cocktails, local brews from the outdoor bar and live music setting the tone, it's a dining experience like no other.

Live Music Venue

Darwin's award-winning live music venue has a 450pax capacity and a distinctive atmosphere. We're not bound by genre and are known to work with a 'broad church' of styles and artists.

The spaces

  • Band Room: a refurbished wartime hut for 250, with a distinctive river bar, green room and efficient air conditioning for even the hottest Darwin days
  • Al Fresco stage: a tropical outdoor setting for 150 people, with standing and smoking areas and a seated restaurant area
  • Back Bar: an intimate space for 70 with capable aircon, popular with singer songwriters, spoken word and vinyl selectors/DJs

Comfortable lounges are placed throughout the venue, and our extensive drink menu means there's something for everyone.

For artists

We love to collaborate and help produce the room. Over 3000 customers come through the venue each week, and we promote each gig across street press, social media, posters and radio. Our ticketing is commission free, giving punters and artists better value for money.

We ask artists to send a Facebook tile (1200 x 628 pixels), an Instagram tile (1080 x 1080 pixels), a bio under 100 words and links to media content such as Soundcloud or Spotify. We also expect artists to promote gigs on their own social media and to be available for media interviews. Printed A4 or A3 posters are encouraged.

Bustard Town is flexible with the venue deal. We aim for fair.

  • Option 1, just roll up and play: we can do ticketing, provide stage tech, back line, sound person, door and security, and help with promo. 75/25 door split
  • Option 2, DJs/bands take the door: we provide the space, you manage the door and pay for sound
