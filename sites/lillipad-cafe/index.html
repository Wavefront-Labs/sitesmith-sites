Lillipad Cafe sits on Spence Street in Cairns City, serving fresh local breakfasts, hearty lunches and perfectly brewed coffee. Grab a comfy spot, order something good and let the morning unfold. No rush, no pressure, just good coffee and better company.

Whether you're popping in for a quick flat white, settling in with the laptop or catching up with mates over lunch, there's a spot here with your name on it. Dine in, sit outside, take away or order delivery, with vegetarian options on the menu.

Made with Love

Every plate is made with care, using fresh local ingredients and a whole lot of love. Good food doesn't need shortcuts. We pick the best ingredients we can get our hands on and let them do the talking.

Our kitchen takes pride in serving food that tastes like home: quality cafe cooking, made for our Cairns community.
