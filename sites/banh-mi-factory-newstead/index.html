Our Story

In 2014, the Banh Mi Factory was brought to life as a market stall at the Eagle Farm Markets. We had a vision to bring the flavours of our favourite Vietnamese street foods to Brisbane.

Today we're still true to our market roots and have been a favourite food destination at the Carseldine Markets, in the north of Brisbane. The success of the market stall, and strong demand for the best and most satisfying banh mi, led us to open our very first store in Zillmere in 2019, serving our traditional family recipes and providing catering for meetings, functions and special events.
