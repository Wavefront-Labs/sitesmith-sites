Taste of Msia is a spacious Malaysian restaurant at 86 Scarborough St, Southport, right next to the Cecil Hotel. Guests come for the authentic flavours, generous portions and a warm, friendly welcome.

Regulars recommend the Claypot Pearl Noodle, the Beef Rendang and the Kangkung Sambal, washed down with a refreshing iced tea. Dine in with us, grab takeaway, or have it delivered.
