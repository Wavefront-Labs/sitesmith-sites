Lumen Cafe is a modern, welcoming cafe at 38 High St, Toowong. It's an easy place to relax, catch up with friends or settle in for a good conversation over coffee, and guests often mention the friendly welcome from owner Keidi.

Breakfast favourites like the smashed avo and the generous Nordic Smash keep people coming back. You can dine in, sit outside, grab takeaway or order delivery. Bookings are welcome and there are vegetarian options.
