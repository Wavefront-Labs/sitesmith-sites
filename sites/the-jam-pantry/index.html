The Jam Pantry is a bright, relaxed neighbourhood cafe on Logan Road in Greenslopes, with beadboard walls and a chalkboard menu of seasonal plates. Housemade jams, pickles, ferments and preserves have long been the Pantry's secret weapon.

We're open 7 days a week, 7am to 2:30pm, for dine-in, takeaway, pick up and Uber. Bookings are welcome, and there's outdoor seating and plenty of vegetarian options.

The Flipping Boards

We build a list of complementary ingredients on a whim, based on what our local farmers and suppliers have harvested and collected. Each item on the boards is then designed around beautiful seasonal produce.

Our current flip is available for dine-in and takeaway. To see our boards coming to life, search #whentheboardflips on Instagram or Facebook, and sign up to our newsletter for recipes, news and easy inspiration.

Our Story

First, a big thank you to the former owners, Gideon and Nims, who ran The Jam Pantry so attentively for years. I'm Victor, the new owner, and I've been part of Greenslopes since 8 April 2020.

The Jam Pantry still looks the way you remember, and most of the menu and drinks will be familiar. We'll also regularly launch new dishes, drinks and desserts, with carefully selected ingredients so everything is fresh and delicious. We kept all of our staff, and I'm very grateful they chose to stay and help write this new chapter.

The Jam Pantry continues our journey of exploring our emotional connection to food. If you'd like to know more, come and see us. We'd love to meet you.

Functions

We love giving you any excuse to get together, and there's nothing quite like a good chat and beautiful treats to make a gathering sweeter. A function at The Jam Pantry can be as simple or as grand as you like (well, within reason).

We've hosted baby showers, birthday parties, Christmas parties and dinner gatherings. There are several options to choose from, priced from $50 to $70 per head, with lots of lovely extras to make your gathering even more special.

We're not a licensed venue, but we're more than happy to accommodate your very important need for champagne. Feel free to BYO. There's an additional charge of $2 per person to cover all your ice and stemware.

All that's left to do is email us for all the delicious details.
