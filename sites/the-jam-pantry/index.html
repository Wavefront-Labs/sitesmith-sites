Our Story

First of all, we’d like to thank the former owners Gideon and Nims, their attentively run The Jam Pantry over the past years.

Well hello!! Thanks for being here!💕 so... what exactly is The Jam Pantry now?? I’m glad you asked......

The Jam Pantry has just been transferred to me. Even so, it still retains its previous appearance. Most of the menus and drinks are familiar to you! Of course, we will also regularly launch new menus, drinks and desserts! We will carefully select the ingredients to ensure that the food is fresh and delicious, to fit your taste!
