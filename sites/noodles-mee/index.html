Noodles Mee is an Asian restaurant at b5/56 Scarborough St, Southport. Diners rate it 4.8 from 171 reviews. Singaporean diners say its laksa and soy sauce noodles are as close as it gets to the real thing on the Gold Coast.

Our laksa is known for its good fragrance and coconut milk, and it is just the thing on a cold night. Come in and dine with Chinese and Cantonese music playing in the background, or order takeaway or delivery.
