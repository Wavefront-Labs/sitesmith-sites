Kaviina is a bright, clean and comfortable cafe at 252 Chapel Rd, Bankstown. The menu leans towards lighter, healthier options with plenty for vegetarians, and the coffee is made with Paradox beans. Our gluten free desserts have become a favourite with locals.

Dine in, grab a takeaway or order delivery. When the evening comes, our outdoor seating is a lovely spot to catch up with friends over a coffee and something sweet.
