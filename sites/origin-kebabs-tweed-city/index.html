About Origin Kebabs

Our Story

Origin Kebabs are one of the healthiest alternatives meals in a food court.

Unlike most, our core products are prepared by our Central Kitchen in Yatala, Gold Coast. It is accredited with SGS HACCAP World Standard certifications.

Our Central Kitchen suppliers are of the highest quality, reputable and use trusted brands like Inghams and Steggles. We use only low fat quality 100% Australian beef and lamb.

Our system of service, is one of the easiest, quick serve kebab outlets in the country. Our smiles and laughter demonstrates the confidence in our product and service. Fast, super friendly service and fresh product is the key to keeping customers frequent.

History of the name

Back in late 80’s Origin Kebabs founder was offered a store at Hyperdome Shopping Centre in Loganholme. For the life of him he couldn’t decide what to name the store. Being a huge Soccer fan Ali didn’t really know much about any other sport. While in QLD inspecting the site, all he heard about was this very popular game called the State Of Origin.

Ali was amazed at the intense popularity of the game. So he thought, why not…and named the first store Origin Kebabs.

FILLA FOOD GROUP

Food Manufacturing.

At Filla Foods, we are proud to manufacture chicken and döner products for our sister company Origin Kebabs. We are passionate about providing quality, authentic products to our valued restaurant and food outlet customers. Since our inception in 2012, we have been a premium one-stop wholesale supplier of Turkish food goods and packaging. With kebabs at the heart of our business, we sell everything from fresh and frozen meat and poultry to sauces, breads, condiments, containers, cleaning implements and of course, döners.

A family business – and the largest of it’s kind in Queensland, we have brought together generations of expertise in our industry to ensure that we have a production process that is second to none. We have our own transportation network and deliver by our own capacity. Filla Foods is a Safe Food accredited food supplier and have HACCP program in place. All the products produced and supplied by Filla Foods are Halal and our business is Halal Certified.

  • Founded in the late 1980s at Hyperdome Shopping Centre, Loganholme QLD

  • Growth journey: From a single store to a nationally recognised franchise built on authentic Turkish food
  • Brand identity: Radiant purple, vibrant orange, and standout yellow signage projecting vibrance, energy, and a modern lively feel.
