Origin Kebabs Tweed City serves classic kebab wraps, kebab burgers, plates and HSPs, plus falafel and veggie options, at 54 Minjungbal Dr, Tweed Heads South. Dine in, grab it as takeaway or have it delivered.

Our regulars come back for fresh salad, generous fillings and good value. We're in a food court, so lunchtime gets busy, but our service is fast and friendly and every kebab is rolled by hand. It's how we roll!

Our Food

Kebabs are the ultimate comfort food, and they're at the heart of everything we do. Our food is locally produced and nutritionally balanced.

Our core products are prepared in our Central Kitchen in Yatala on the Gold Coast, which holds SGS HACCP World Standard certifications. Our suppliers use trusted brands like Inghams and Steggles, and we use only low fat, quality, 100% Australian beef and lamb.

Our Story

In the late 80s, Origin Kebabs founder Ali Engin was offered a store at Hyperdome Shopping Centre in Loganholme. For the life of him he couldn't decide what to name it.

Ali was a huge soccer fan who didn't know much about other sports. While he was in Queensland inspecting the site, all he heard about was a very popular game called the State of Origin. He was amazed by how intensely popular it was, so he thought, why not? He named the first store Origin Kebabs.

Our system makes us one of the easiest quick-serve kebab outlets in the country. Our smiles and laughter show how confident we are in our food and service. Fast, super friendly service and fresh product keep our customers coming back.

Filla Foods

Our sister company, Filla Foods, makes the chicken and döner products for Origin Kebabs. Since starting in 2012, Filla Foods has been a premium one-stop wholesale supplier of Turkish food goods and packaging.

Filla Foods is a family business and the largest of its kind in Queensland. It brings together generations of industry expertise and runs its own transport network. It is a Safe Food accredited supplier with a HACCP program in place. All of its products are Halal, and the business is Halal Certified.
