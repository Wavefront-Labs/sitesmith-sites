Hush Specialty Coffee is a relaxed, welcoming cafe at 68 High St, right in the heart of Fremantle. We serve good coffee alongside breakfast, pastries, bagels and croissants.

Pull up a seat inside or outdoors, or grab your order to take away. Vegetarian options are available.
