At Ostro Eatery we believe in the power of good coffee and warm hospitality. Our journey began with a simple vision: to create a welcoming space where friends, families and strangers alike could come together to enjoy delicious drinks, homemade treats and meaningful connections.

With a prime oceanview spot on the Rockingham Foreshore, Ostro brings vibrant energy and heartfelt hospitality to every visit. Whether you're after a leisurely brunch or just soaking up the seaside vibes, you'll find a warm, bright and friendly atmosphere here.
