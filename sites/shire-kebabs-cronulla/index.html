Shire Kebabs is a local kebab shop at 58 Cronulla St, Cronulla. Locals keep coming back for the tender, well-seasoned meat, generous portions and friendly service, and they've given us a 4.5 star rating from 459 reviews.

Eat in, grab a seat outside, take it away or get it delivered. However you have it, it's made fresh and made with care.
