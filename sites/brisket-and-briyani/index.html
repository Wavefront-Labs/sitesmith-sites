Our Story

Brisket & Briyani was born from a simple obsession: what happens when you marry 12-hour Texas-style smoked meats with authentic Chennai-style South Indian briyani (biryani, as most of Brisbane spells it), crafted from scratch every week?

No shortcuts. No processed ingredients. Every brisket smoked low and slow. Every pot of briyani built with whole spices and long-grain basmati. We believe fast food should mean fresh food, served quickly, not food that sat in a factory.

From our first Saturday in Sunnybank to our growing family of locations, the mission stays the same: real food, real fast, no compromise.
