Since 2017, The Dry Leaf Cafe has been serving Mosman with a passion for hospitality and a love of beautiful, quality coffee. Our menu is simple, healthy and delicious, inspired by a fusion of authentic Asian (Nepalese) and modern Australian flavours.

You'll find us at 26 Spit Road, Mosman, right next to the B1 bus stop heading northbound. Locals love our warm, cosy, peaceful vibe, and our team takes pride in food that looks good and tastes amazing.

About Us

Our cafe has something for everyone, including gluten-free and vegan cakes, pies and sandwiches, along with hot food, cold brews, cold drinks, freshly squeezed juices, smoothies and more.

Proudly serving Sacred Ground Coffee

We proudly serve Sacred Ground, a 100% certified organic and fair-trade coffee. We take our coffee very seriously and use the highest grade beans to get the best brews and the best aromatic coffee flavour. Sacred Ground's coffee and roasting facility is certified organic by Australian Certified Organic (ACO).

Organic and fair-trade coffee has been at the heart of our cafe from the beginning. We believe it's a better choice for the planet, for communities and for all of us coffee lovers.

Our food

We serve breakfast and lunch on an all-day menu, with gluten-free, vegetarian, dairy-free and vegan options. We believe fresh, unprocessed, quality food makes for an exciting, delicious and heartwarming menu, and we make as many of our ingredients in the cafe as we can for that home-made taste.

Some of our favourite dishes include our gluten-free, vegan buckwheat pancake, the Mountain Paleo, traditional mountain curries, dumplings, cumin beef burger and seafood chowder.
