Cheek Cafe is a local cafe at 1 Como Cres, Southport, with dine-in, takeaway and outdoor seating. Bookings are welcome.

Regulars love the proper table service, with no QR codes in sight, and the sunny welcome from owner Natalie and her team. Guests describe the food as organic and clearly made with love, and plenty of them keep coming back.
