Founded in 2022, fifi's by the beach is a place to enjoy food, coffee and wine in a comfortable, friendly and relaxed atmosphere, set beside beautiful Williamstown Beach.

Find us on the corner at 81 Esplanade, Williamstown, right across from the water. Come in for a late breakfast, a long brunch or lunch, and take in the view.
