Cafe Gold Coast is a buzzing, rustic cafe on Marine Parade in Labrador, with timber walls and outdoor tables by the water. We serve breakfasts, sandwiches and burgers, with vegetarian options too.

Dine in, grab takeaway, or bring the dog and sit outside, where pets are welcome. Our regulars come back for consistently good coffee and food, generous portions and very reasonable prices.
