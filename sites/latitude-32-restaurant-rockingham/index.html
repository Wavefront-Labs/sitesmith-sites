Exceptional steak, fresh seafood and a table by the water. Latitude 32 sits on The Boardwalk on the Rockingham Foreshore, with plenty of seating inside and out, including a terrace overlooking the water.

From the first oyster to the last sip, settle in for a meal shaped by quality steaks, fresh seafood and the beauty of the Rockingham coast. Dine in, book a table, or order takeaway or delivery. Vegetarian options are available too.

Our story

Latitude 32 was opened in March 2014 by Marc and Narelle Kenly.

Marc has spent the better part of 30 years in hospitality, mostly in restaurants and catering, and specifically in steak houses in Canada. He started out as an entrepreneur in Rockingham in 2010, opening takeaway venues including Oceanview Seafoods and Dhuey's Fish Market, before going on to open Latitude 32.

Since then Latitude 32 has won many awards in Licensed Casual Dining and Best Steak House across the Perth metro area. The most notable is the Gold Plate award for Best Steak House in WA 2017. Every one of them comes from the hard work and dedication of our front of house team and the chefs in our kitchen.
