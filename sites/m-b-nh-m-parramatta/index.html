Mê Bánh Mì is a Vietnamese bánh mì shop at Suite 3/52 George St, Parramatta. Our rolls come generously filled, and the crispy pork roll has become a firm local favourite.

Eat in with us, grab a roll to take away, or have it delivered. We're rated 4.4 from 167 Google reviews, and we're grateful to every customer who takes the time to tell us how we're going.
