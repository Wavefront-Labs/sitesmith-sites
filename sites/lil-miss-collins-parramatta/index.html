Lil Miss Collins is a cafe at 13 Wentworth St, Parramatta, with a relaxed vibe and a team guests describe as super friendly and welcoming. Dine in, sit outside, grab takeaway or have it delivered, and book ahead if you'd like a table.

There are vegetarian options, and our staff are happy to take the time to talk you through any dietary requirements. Regulars rate the pancakes, the hash browns and the big breaky, served with sourdough that's lightly buttered and toasted just right.
