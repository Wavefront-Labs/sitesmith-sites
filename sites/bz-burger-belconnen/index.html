Our story began in Kippax in 2017, and BZ Burger quickly became a local favourite for its mouth-watering burgers. We still make our beef patties, sauces, pickles, fried chicken, shakes and desserts by hand.

We're all about fun, tasty food, and we keep adding new twists to our burgers to keep things interesting. Our menu takes its energy from Canberra's streets and keeps evolving with the latest food trends. Come in and see why we're one of Canberra's favourites.

Food truck and coffee cart hire

Looking for something extra special? Our food truck is available for events and private catering. With our mobile kitchen, we can bring the BZB experience to you, wherever you are.
