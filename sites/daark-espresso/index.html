Daark is an espresso bar known for exceptional coffee, warm service and standout food. Tucked away in the charming pocket of Chirn Park on the Northern Gold Coast, it's open seven days for dine-in and takeaway, with all-day breakfast, lunch, beer, wine and cocktails. Judging by the regular queue for a table, it's clearly a local favourite.

The menu runs from Sweet Potato and Feta Fritters to Turkish Eggs and Lamb Salad, all paired with award-winning Supreme Roasters coffee. Try the Biscoff latte, a rotating selection of matchas (including strawberry, blackberry and classic ceremonial), and don't skip the homemade vanilla cold foam. Trust us. Struggling to leave your cosy bed? We provide warm woolly wraps to snuggle into while you dine alfresco on the wrap-around deck.

Book a table

We're a full table service venue, so we kindly ask that you wait at the bottom of the stairs to be seated. Our team will get you settled as soon as possible.

To book a table for 4 or more, call or text us on 0450 988 899 during our opening times (5.30am to 2.30pm).

  • We accept reservations for groups of 4 to 10 people.
  • No same-day bookings.
  • We don't take bookings on public holidays or the Gold Coast Marathon weekend.
  • We hold the table for 15 minutes past your reservation time. After that, it may be given to another customer.
  • Tables are reserved for 1.5 hours. If you'd like to stay longer, please let us know when you book.
