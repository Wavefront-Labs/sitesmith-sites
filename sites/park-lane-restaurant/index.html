Park Lane Restaurant serves modern Australian food made with fresh, seasonal, locally sourced ingredients. Whether you're after steak, seafood or something lighter, we do it all, in a small, well-run dining room at the back of EconoLodge Park Lane on Bourbong Street. We're a favourite with locals and visitors alike, and a proud recipient of the Choice Gold Hospitality Award, which recognises excellence in quality, service and guest satisfaction.

We're independently owned and run by the Ward family. Greg, a chef by trade, has spent 30 years in hospitality, 20 of them at Park Lane: first as the chef, then as owner of the restaurant for 15 years, and now as owner of the whole motel. Chef Aaron cooks our award-winning food for guests and visiting diners, and Jonathan manages the restaurant to make sure your night out is a memorable one.

Dinner at Park Lane

Join us for dinner from 6pm to 8pm, Monday to Thursday. You can dine in or take away, and we have vegetarian options.

Bookings are welcome. Call us on (07) 4151 2341.

You'll find us at 247 Bourbong St, Bundaberg, in the EconoLodge Park Lane. We're just across the road from Alexandra Park and the Burnett River, in the Bundaberg CBD.

We also cater to the Chalet Motor Inn, Bundaberg City Motor Inn and Acacia Motor Inn.
