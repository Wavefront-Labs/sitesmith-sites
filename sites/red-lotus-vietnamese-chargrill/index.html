Red Lotus was established in 2010 by chef and owner Leighton Rossi. Together with his sister Margaret Nguyen of Cafe O Mai, both stores have built a reputation as some of the most popular and busiest Vietnamese food spots in Brisbane.

Our cosy restaurant on Cracknell Road, with its colourful lights and rich wooden decor, is a relaxed place for a casual dinner with family and friends. Dine in, take away or order delivery.

The Food

Red Lotus offers a full Vietnamese dining experience, with food strongly influenced by the southern Vietnamese style of cooking. Chef Leighton Rossi heads the kitchen, and the menu covers a wide collection of Vietnamese favourites, including an exclusive charcoal menu and an ever-expanding vegan range.

Guests regularly mention our generous portions and reasonable prices, and the vegan pho and salt and pepper crispy tofu are favourites. We are happy to look after dietary requirements, with plenty of vegetarian and vegan options.
