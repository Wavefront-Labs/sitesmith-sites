Laneway Coffee is a bright, airy cafe in Parap Village with an exposed-brick feature wall, good coffee, thoughtful food and familiar faces. It's where Darwin gathers, whether you're a local regular or just off the plane.

Eat in, sit outside, grab a takeaway or order delivery. We serve an all-day menu with vegetarian options and a kids menu, so the whole crew is looked after.
