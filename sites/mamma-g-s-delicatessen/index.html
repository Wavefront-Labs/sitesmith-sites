About

Mamma G's is an Italian corner deli, cafe and pantry on Boundary Street in the heart of Coolangatta. Handmade pasta, antipasto, house-made sauces and focaccia straight from the oven, seven days a week, from 5:30am to 7:00pm.

Charcuterie and salumi, fresh pasta and slow-simmered sauces, and the pantry staples of good Italian home cooking, side by side with the best artisanal local, regional and national produce. A place to grab a coffee. To collect dinner. To bring a little Calabria home with you.

Just like Mamma makes

Mamma G's is a tribute to Joyce Gambaro. To the Gambaro family, she is Mamma. To Coolangatta, she is the reason this deli exists.

The Gambaro story begins in 1937 in Falerna, a small village in Calabria, Italy, where Michael Gambaro, father to John, Frank and Donny, was born. In 1950, at just thirteen years old, Michael made the long journey to Australia and settled in Brisbane, bringing his family's recipes with him and their traditions in his hands. In 1953 he bought a shop on Caxton Street, and what began there has grown, over seven decades, into one of Australia's most respected hospitality families.

But before all of that, and behind all of it, there has always been the family kitchen. And in the family kitchen, there has always been Joyce. Mamma G's is her deli: a small, honest neighbourhood store where the food is made with the same care she pours into every meal she cooks. Mamma taught us how. This is our way of saying thank you.

The deli counter

The heart of Mamma G's. Artisan cheeses, premium salumi, antipasto, prepared salads and ready-to-enjoy meals, sliced and served to order. Carefully selected local and imported products sit alongside house-made favourites, for everyday shopping and effortless entertaining.

Tell the team what you are feeding and how many, and they will cut it, weigh it and wrap it while you wait. Ask for a slice before you buy. That is what the counter is for.

Pantry, produce and focaccia

Curated shelves of Italian and Australian favourites: premium olive oils, artisan pasta, biscuits, chocolates, preserves and specialty ingredients. Every product is chosen to inspire home cooks and food lovers alike.

Inspired by the food markets of Europe, the produce section brings fresh fruit, vegetables, herbs and everyday essentials, selected for quality, flavour and freshness.
