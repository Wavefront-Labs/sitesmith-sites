Thai Paragon has been serving authentic, traditional Thai cuisine since 2008. With more than 10 years in the industry, we're a well-known Thai restaurant in New South Wales, and you'll find us at Shop 2/292 Chapel Rd, Bankstown.

Come in and dine with us, grab takeaway, or book a table. Guests often mention our friendly, attentive staff and dishes full of flavour, with the whole fish and chicken fried rice among the favourites.
