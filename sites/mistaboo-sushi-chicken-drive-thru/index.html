Our Story

MistaBoo is a Japanese restaurant bringing fresh sushi and everyday Japanese favourites to communities across Brisbane and beyond. Since 2022, we've focused on one simple idea: making good Japanese food easy to enjoy.

From freshly made rolls to nigiri, aburi, sashimi, rice bowls, udon, ramen and hot dishes, our menu is made for everyone to find something they love. Whether it's a quick lunch, dinner with family or an easy meal with friends, MistaBoo is here to make every visit fresh, relaxed and enjoyable.

What Our Customers Love

  • Katsu curry with a good-sized schnitzel and plenty of rice

Part of a Growing Family

MistaBoo began in Brisbane in 2022 and has grown to locations across Queensland and New South Wales, each built around the same commitment to quality, consistency and customer experience.

Logan Central marks the next step in the growth of our franchise network. Our team worked closely with our franchise partner through planning, setup, training and preparation for opening, building on the experience and systems developed across our existing stores.

Interested in opening a MistaBoo in your community? Submit an enquiry and our team will be in touch to discuss the next steps.
