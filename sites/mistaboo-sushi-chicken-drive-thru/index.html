OUR STORY

Fresh Sushi. More Choice. More Fun.

MistaBoo is a Japanese restaurant bringing fresh sushi and everyday Japanese favourites to communities across Brisbane and beyond. Since 2022, we’ve focused on one simple idea — making good Japanese food easy to enjoy.

From sushi on train and freshly made rolls to nigiri, aburi, sashimi, rice bowls, udon, ramen and hot dishes, our menu is made for everyone to find something they love. Whether it’s a quick lunch, dinner with family or an easy meal with friends, MistaBoo is here to make every visit fresh, relaxed and enjoyable.

Pick What You Love

Our Sushi on Train experience brings a wide variety of freshly prepared sushi straight past your table, so you can choose exactly what you feel like, when you feel like it. From classic favourites to MistaBoo creations, there’s always something new coming around. Fresh choices, easy dining and plenty to discover — that’s MistaBoo.
