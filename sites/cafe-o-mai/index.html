O-Mai translates as "dried apricot" or "teenager" in Vietnamese, and Cafe O-Mai (pronounced Cafe Oh My) is a little quirky, intimate Vietnamese cafe in Annerley. We're nestled in an historic corner shop away from the hustle and bustle of Ipswich Rd, near Chardon's Corner. It's a safe place to catch up with an old friend or have a good cup of coffee with the paper.

Our menu reflects our Vietnamese heritage, with some western influence in our breakfast menu. It's not fusion, so don't get it confused! Yes, you can have a bowl of pho (pronounced "fur") at 7am. In the streets of Vietnam, vendors sell banh mi and steaming hot soups for breakfast, so get your pho on and head to Cafe O-Mai for something different and special.

Our Food

We celebrate our heritage with authentic Vietnamese food. It's simple, uncomplicated, home style comfort food, known for its subtle clean flavours and farm fresh produce. Everything is MSG free.

A major kitchen upgrade in 2015 lets us make everything in house, from our pho broth to our freshly baked banh mi, lemongrass pork sausages, spring rolls and dim sim. Our slices, cakes, muffins and biscuits are baked on the premises too.

We're proud of our vegan and gluten free options, and we don't discriminate on dietary needs. If you're gluten or dairy intolerant, we can cater for you. We don't judge on your budget either: our menu fits everyone's hip pocket.

To go with your meal, we have quality espresso coffee, traditional Vietnamese coffee dripped through filters, and fresh cold pressed juices.

Menu prices may change without notice. Please check the current menu for prices at the time of purchase.

Bookings and Walk-ins

We take limited bookings, and walk-ins are welcome on a first in, first served basis. We don't take bookings close to the time of arrival.

For bookings, please call 07 3255 9778 to confirm. If you're pre-ordering for a large booking by email, please call to confirm we've received your order.

Order Online

You can order and pay for takeaway online. Orders take about 20 minutes on average. During peak times and weekends this may vary, so please call us to check.

  • There's no confirmation email. Just save your order number, go to the little takeaway window and mention it to collect your order.
  • You can leave your name in the notes for easier pickup.
  • Chargrilled dishes are available after 11:15am. If you pre-order these, please note your pickup time.
  • For large orders, please order by phone or in store.
