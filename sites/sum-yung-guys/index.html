Sum Yung Guys serves innovative Asian-fusion small and share plates in an airy space with a cool street-food vibe. The food is an expression of everything we have come to love about the region, and the cocktails have been developed to complement it.

Find us at Unit 1/205 Weyba Rd, Noosaville, for dine in, takeaway or a long lunch out on our outdoor seating. Vegetarian options are on the menu, and a few dishes come with a chilli warning. We mean it.

SYG Take Home Curry Sauces

Our take home curry sauces are back. Craving your favourite curry from Sum Yung Guys? Now you can enjoy it anytime, right from the comfort of your own home.

Our yellow, green and red curry sauces are crafted with the same bold flavours and quality ingredients you know and love from our kitchen. They're available exclusively at our Noosa restaurant, so drop by, stock up and take the flavour home with you.
