Overlooking one of Dandenong's most iconic buildings, the Drum Theatre, and set in the new Dandenong Council Chambers, The Public's Corner offers a relaxed, vibrant atmosphere with a lively mix of food, wine and cakes. Enjoy the open-air outdoor seating area or dine inside in our restaurant.

We're a fully licensed venue with vegetarian options, and we offer dine in, takeaway and bookings. Guests often tell us about our brunches, and families and big groups alike feel right at home.

Open for breakfast and lunch seven days a week, variety is the key ingredient of our internationally inspired menu. From hearty breakfasts to casual bites and à la carte restaurant fare, our well-crafted menu offers something for everyone.

Our fresh, delicious food is matched with some of Australia's best boutique wines, as well as a variety of beers and ciders.
