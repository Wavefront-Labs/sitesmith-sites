About

Tikka Taco Day is a North Parramatta restaurant at 144a Pennant St, serving bold, nicely spiced food for dine in or takeaway, with vegetarian options. Regulars keep coming back for the Chicken Tikka Skewers, Chicken Tikka Quesadilla, Chicken Tikka Burrito and Masala Fries.

With a 4.5 rating from 271 Google reviews, locals praise the food as flavourful, fresh and consistent, with just the right amount of kick. Reviewers also mention fast, efficient service, even when things get busy.
