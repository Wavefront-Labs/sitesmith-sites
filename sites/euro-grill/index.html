About Us

Who We Are Offering Fresh Food, Fresh Vibes At Euro Grill, we believe great food has the power to bring people together, creating moments filled with laughter, connection, and comfort in a welcoming space designed for everyone to enjoy. Located at Burleigh Waters, we pride ourselves on delivering consistent quality, friendly service, and a vibrant atmosphere that keeps both locals and visitors coming back regularly. Our organic, single-origin coffee is locally roasted in Burleigh Heads, offering rich flavour and freshness in every cup. Paired perfectly with our Mediterranean-inspired dishes crafted for modern Australian tastes, it’s the ideal choice for breakfast, lunch, or a relaxed afterno

Gourmet Flavours Always

Creative menus crafted to satisfy every palate with memorable flavours.

Friendly Customer Service

Euro Grill Story

Our menu celebrates variety and flavour, from highly enjoyed breakfast favourites to gourmet burgers that stand apart, offering unique combinations and bold tastes that have made us the best restaurant and fine dinner spot that Burleigh and Gold Coast locals trust.
