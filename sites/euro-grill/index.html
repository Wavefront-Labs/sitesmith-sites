At Euro Grill, we bring people together through flavour, warmth and great company, from relaxed breakfasts to vibrant dinners in a welcoming, modern setting. We focus on quality ingredients, consistent service and genuine hospitality, and our organic, single-origin coffee is locally roasted in Burleigh Heads.

We're open for breakfast and lunch daily from 6 AM to 2 PM, and for dinner on Friday and Saturday from 5 PM to 9 PM. Dine in, sit outside, grab takeaway or have it delivered, and there are vegetarian options right across the menu.
