Estabar is a compact beachfront cafe at 61 Shortland Esplanade, Newcastle Beach. Expect salty air and ocean views, seriously good coffee, house made seasonal gelato and a menu parading our local fruit and vegetables.

We serve classic breakfasts, single-origin coffee and hot chocolate. Dine in, grab takeaway or settle in outside, and there are vegetarian options too.

Our Community

We work with change-makers, thought provokers, the old-school and the new: people who are stubborn about the integrity of their craft and proud of what they do.

The beautiful, nourishing experiences you have at Estabar come from their care for their work and the environments they operate in.

Stories

We hope you've noticed that a big concern for us is the future of food, which is why we work with the people we do.

Dave

Dave collects green waste from businesses like Estabar, including our spent coffee, as well as from homes around Newcastle. He takes it back to his family property on the lush banks of the Allyn River and returns it to earth. When the compost becomes sweet-smelling dirt again, he brings it to one of his urban farms, unused plots around the city, and grows veggies. He then sells them at Newcastle Farmers Market.

Dave also wants to help you connect with your food and the earth. His 1 Hour Farmer program lets anyone spend 1 hour on one of the farms, getting dirty, happy and inspired.

Sarah

Dave and his sister Sarah are 5th generation farmers. They're choosing to stay on the land, keep producing food for our plates and farm in a way that regenerates and sustains their fields. We first fell for Sarah because of her palpable passion for agriculture that grows good food in respectful and resourceful collaboration with the earth and animals.

When she started with 500 ladies and offered eggs delivered a day or two after lay, we were interested. Then we heard about her making splints for chooks' legs, sleeping under their caravans at night to make sure the fans ran properly, sitting with them for hours a day and buying all the ice around to keep them cool on hot days. We were hooked. She is all heart, and we can't describe how much we love her and her eggs.

Little Big Dairy Co

In 2019 we switched to Little Big Dairy Co for our milk and cream. This family operation, located out past Dubbo, manages their milk from the paddock all the way to your cup, so they can charge a fair price and reinvest in their farm, their cows and their future. Each of their 800 cows is known by name, and an Ag Nutritionist designs a supplementary feed menu for each cow according to its health and the grasses available in the paddocks.

Sam

Sam makes beautiful green bouquets, and visiting him at the Farmers Market is a joy. He is wonderfully present. Often he picks out a perfectly ripe fig, a piece of mandarin or some other sweet treat from his orchard and says 'this is for you.' He is always positive and pragmatic. Find Sam from Mangrove Mountain at the Farmers Market.

Maggie

Maggie is a chef and one of only two professional Juice Therapists in Australia. Yep, juice therapy is a thing. Maggie works with all kinds of people facing all kinds of health challenges, helping them heal their bodies with nutrients found in fresh fruit and vegetables.
