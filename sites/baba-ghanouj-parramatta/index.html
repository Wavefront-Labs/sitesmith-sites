Baba Ghanouj Parramatta is a Lebanese restaurant and event venue on Phillip Street, where tradition, flavour and hospitality come together. Dining with us feels like dining at home, with a warm, family-friendly atmosphere and authentic food made with love. Our kitchen runs all day, from breakfast through lunch and dinner.

Everything is prepared in-house each day, from vibrant mezza to charcoal-grilled meats and fresh seafood, with plenty of vegetarian dishes and warm house-made bread. Our name comes from the iconic dish of eggplant charred over an open flame, then lifted with olive oil and lemon. It stands for the simplicity, care and depth of flavour that guide our cooking.

About Us

Our menu is made for sharing, with generous portions whether you are joining us for a casual meal or a special occasion. For the full experience, our banquet menus are an easy way to enjoy the best of what we do.

To go with your meal, we offer a thoughtful selection of wines, beers and other drinks, including specialty imports from Lebanon.

We aim to create more than just a meal. We want it to feel familiar, generous and memorable. We look forward to welcoming you.
