Salt & Pepper Vietnamese Kitchen was established in 2018 by the previous owners of a successful Vietnamese restaurant in Adelaide. We set out to bring the same wholesome experience to the heart of the Norwood Parade, at 2/1 Margaret St.

Come in and be immersed in authenticity and delight as we serve you food made pho the soul. Dine in, grab takeaway or have it delivered.

Something for Everyone

We do our best to cater for our diverse range of customers. Our menu offers the following options:

  • Vegetarian
  • Vegan
  • Halal
  • Gluten-free
  • Lactose-free

We believe authentic flavour can be drawn from our fresh produce without the unnecessary stuff. Everything we offer is low in MSG, and we have some MSG-free options too, if that's what floats ya boat.

Bookings and Takeaway

Bookings and takeaway orders are taken over the phone.

Please make a booking if you have a group of 4 or more. We don't want to turn you away once our cosy space starts filling up!

Catering

Did you know we offer catering? Our entire menu is fine for catering.

Just give us the details at least 2 days beforehand and we'll make it happen. Call us or email saltandpeppernorwood@gmail.com.
