Seafood market at CALOUNDRA offers the finest quality fresh product – straight from the trawler to you. Situated in the heart of Caloundra, we have a plentiful supply of seafood delicacies ranging from fresh to cooked, to appetising salads and accompaniments, including all of your old favourites, and now offering you our new Chargrilled & Oyster Bar section.
