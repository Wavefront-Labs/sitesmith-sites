About

  • Dine in
  • Takeaway
  • Outdoor seating

Seafood Market @ CALOUNDRA offers the finest quality fresh product, straight from the trawler to you. Right in the heart of Caloundra on Bulcock Street, we're a fresh seafood market and a traditional fish and chippery under one roof.

We have a plentiful supply of seafood, from fresh to cooked, plus appetising salads and accompaniments, all of your old favourites, and our Chargrilled & Oyster Bar section. Eat in at our outdoor tables, or take away and enjoy it at nearby Bulcock Beach.

Fresh Seafood

We carry a full range of fresh local seafood: a large variety of whole fish, many varieties of fish fillets, oysters and a full range of fresh crustaceans.

  • Whole fish
  • Fillets
  • Oysters
  • Prawns
  • Crustaceans

Our friendly staff will clean your chosen seafood at no cost, so you can simply enjoy the pleasures of the ocean. Ask our in-house chefs for advice on how to make the most of fresh seafood in your kitchen.

For a special occasion or to make entertaining easy, seafood platters are available on request.

Freshly Cooked Fish

The traditional fish and chippery is what Seafood Market @ CALOUNDRA has always been about. Enjoy our award-winning fish and chips and extras from our extensive takeaway menu.

Our large range of fish fillets can be crumbed, battered or grilled to your liking, from our Australian, New Zealand and Value selections.

Our By Catch Menu

When our fishing crews are trawling for prawns, their by catch or extras might be bugs or crabs. So you might order a main of fish and chips, then add a scallop or seafood stick from the by catch menu.

Build Your Burger

Build your own burger at our Burger Bar, or try a fish wrap as an alternative. Salads, brown rice and accompaniments are also available.

Seafood Packs and Meal Deals

Seafood Market @ CALOUNDRA boasts the Sunshine Coast's largest range of cooked seafood packs for takeaway, with kids, couples and family packs plus meal deals.

Our Famous Chargrill

We pride ourselves on offering you a restaurant meal you can take home. Our team includes qualified restaurant chefs who also happen to be seafood specialists.

Choose a chargrilled skewer, Tasmanian salmon fillet or Moreton Bay bug, and add your choice of brown rice or chips and salad. Or try our signature dish, the Char, Char, Char.

The Oyster Bar

Try our oysters kilpatrick and mornay, and Hervey Bay scallops on the half shell with garlic butter, or with prosciutto, lime and parmesan. All our Oyster Bar selections also feature on our takeaway menu.
