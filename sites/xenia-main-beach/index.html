Xenia is the ancient Greek concept of hospitality: the generosity and courtesy shown to those far from home. We're a family-owned Greek restaurant on Peak Avenue in Main Beach, inspired by Kastellorizo Island, with a sister venue in Coolangatta.

Come in for traditional Greek food, local and Greek wines and beers on tap, and you'll feel like you're on a Greek island. We're open 7 days for dine in and takeaway, with lunch from 12pm to 3pm and dinner from 5pm to 8:30pm (9pm on Friday and Saturday).

Our Values

  • Philoxenia: the Greek word for welcoming even the stranger into your world. When we open our restaurant, we open our hearts.
  • Meraki: the soul, creativity and passion you put into your work. It's the ingredient that sets Xenia apart.
  • Play like Leonidas: lead by example, take ownership and inspire those around you. Actions speak louder than words.
  • Be like Aristotle: be resourceful, insightful and innovative, and solve challenges creatively.
  • Don't be a Malaka: our no BS rule. Talk straight, be honest and build teamwork through 'No triangles'.
