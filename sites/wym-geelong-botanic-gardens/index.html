WYM is Where You Meet, a licensed cafe tucked away in the ferns of the Geelong Botanic Gardens and the first space in our collection. It's Geelong's favourite hideaway, just a 10 minute walk up from Eastern Beach and a short stroll from the CBD.

Come for specialty coffee, local beers and wines and delicious food, all with the most beautiful garden views. Our menu features ingredients sourced from Geelong and surrounding regions. Whether you're here for a morning coffee, a light meal or an afternoon drink, we welcome you to feel right at home.

Takeaway adventures

Grab coffee, pastries and other goodies as you explore Eastern Park. Takeaway starts from 9:30am Monday to Wednesday, 8:30am Thursday to Saturday and 9:30am Sunday.

Finding us

Enter on foot through the Gardens' main gate at the corner of Podbury Drive and Eastern Park Circuit. Follow the path for about 3 minutes and you'll see WYM on your right across the lawn. The Gardens provide disability parking, ramp access to the main gate and accessible toilets.

Where You Meet PTY LTD is an independent business. It is not endorsed by or affiliated with the City of Greater Geelong or the Geelong Botanic Gardens.

Bookings

Planning ahead or just passing by? We love both. Only some of our cafe seating can be booked online. We keep the rest for walk-ins.

Whether you've been exploring the Gardens, cooling off at Eastern Beach or watching the weekend cricket nearby, we'll always do our best to find you a table. If we're at capacity, we'll add you to our short waitlist at the entrance, or you can grab takeaway and enjoy it in your favourite patch of the Gardens. Either way, you'll never be turned away without an option.

A few things to note

  • Bookings of 10 or more guests must book our shared Feed Me Grazing experience.
  • For general bookings, deposits are non-refundable for no-shows or cancellations within 24 hours.
  • We do not offer traditional high tea packages.
  • Cake to celebrate? Absolutely bring one. A $2pp cakeage fee applies.
  • All spaces within the Geelong Botanic Gardens, including WYM, must remain open to the public, even during private bookings.

Functions and Venue Hire

Planning something worth celebrating? WYM is the perfect spot to bring people together over great food, drinks and good energy.

Spaces

  • Half venue: reserve the majority of the outdoor area, extending out from our undercover area.
  • Whole outdoor venue: reserve the entire outdoor venue, including the whole alfresco area with heaters and draw-down blinds, out to the extended outdoors.

Food packages

These packages are available at the Geelong Botanic Gardens venue only. They're designed for sharing and celebrating, with a focus on quality, locally sourced ingredients. Choose a relaxed entrée-style grazing platter, or upgrade to our more substantial grazing experience with warm focaccia, melted brie and hot chorizo as a true centrepiece.

Drinks packages

Our drinks packages range from simple to all-inclusive. Options start with self-serve tea, coffee and juices, move through curated selections of wine and beer, and go up to premium free-flowing packages with cocktails and full beverage access. You can tailor any package to your event and add a bar tab for extra flexibility.

Please note that WYM in the Geelong Botanic Gardens is not the right fit for wedding receptions.

WYM on Wheels Mobile Bar

You enjoy the moment, we'll handle the drinks. WYM on Wheels is our mobile bar caravan, and it comes to you anywhere across Geelong, the Bellarine and the Surf Coast. We serve local beers, wines and made-to-order cocktails at weddings, birthdays, baby showers, engagement parties and other special events.

How it works

  • Choose a set package or fully customisable options.
  • Mobile setup, staff and glassware are all included.
  • We handle the service so you don't have to.
  • Any unused minimum spend can be credited toward another event within 6 months, in any WYM space.

For a simple, stress-free option, tell us your budget and event style and we'll curate the drinks package for you. If you want more flexibility, choose your drinks, inclusions and experience, and we'll build and price it to suit.

Package options

  • Welcome drinks: mimosas or bellinis, sparkling wine, cocktails, wine and beer
  • Coffee and tea self-serve: free-flowing tea and coffee from dispensers, priced per head, per hour
  • Wines, beer and spritz: free-flowing wine with a choice of 3 wines, free-flowing beer with a choice of 2 canned options, and free-flowing Aperol spritz
  • Cocktails and mocktails: one cocktail or mocktail as an arrival drink, or free-flowing. Examples include espresso martini and margarita
  • Alcohol free: pre-paid per head, with juice options and local sparkling or soft drink cans
