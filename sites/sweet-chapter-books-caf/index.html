About

    Sweet Chapter Books & Café was created from a simple belief: life feels richer when you have a good book in one hand and a warm drink in the other. We're a community-minded space where readers, families, creatives and coffee lovers come together.

    Whether you're here to find your next favourite novel, enjoy a slow morning with a cappuccino or join one of our lively events, you'll always feel at home. We're a cosy, judgement-free space built for comfort, kindness and being yourself, and a proud home for indie romance authors.

    Menu

    The Café

    Soft lighting, warm timber and shelves lined with romance novels make a space where you can slow down, sip something sweet and enjoy a moment of calm. Whether you're catching up with a friend or settling in with a book, our café is built for comfort and connection.

    • Locally sourced beans, roasted for rich flavour
    • Hot and cold drinks, snacks and a range of sweet treats
    • Dairy-free milks, gluten-free treats and vegan-friendly options. Just ask our team and we'll help you find something delicious

    Little Chapters for Little Ones

    Babychino: a perfectly sized frothy treat for our youngest visitors, served warm and milky.

    Pupperchinos

    Because every good café adventure should include your four-legged best friend.

    Romance Library

    Explore our ever-expanding library of romance novels. Want a sweet love story or something spicy? Whether you're into small-town romances, enemies-to-lovers or happily-ever-afters, there's a story waiting for you.

    We proudly support indie romance authors with curated shelves, launches and reader discovery. Look out for our blind date with a book section and bookish gifts too.

    Monthly Feature

    Each month we feature a signature café item, plus a book and author of the month.

    Iced Strawberry Matcha

    Our Iced Strawberry Matcha is taking the café by storm. Stop by and try this vibrant drink for yourself, and discover why it's quickly becoming a customer favourite.

    Events

    Community is one of our core values, so we host all sorts of events in a safe, fun space for everyone. From book-themed trivia and book bedazzling to cosy craft nights, we do it all. We also regularly host author meet-and-greets, where you can chat with your favourite writers and finally ask those burning questions.
