For close to 30 years, Mary G’s has been your go-to spot for great food, live entertainment, and unforgettable moments. We’ve cherished every laugh, every meal, and every evening spent with our wonderful community. As we look forward to more incredible years ahead, we are excited to share an important update: Mary G’s is rebranding to Hotel Lismore . While our name is changing, our essence remains the same. We’re still the local pub you’ve known and loved, dedicated to bringing you the best in Australian pub cuisine, live sports, and family-friendly entertainment.
