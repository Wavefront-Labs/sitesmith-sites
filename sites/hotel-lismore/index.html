For close to 30 years, Mary G's has been Lismore's go-to spot for great food, live entertainment and unforgettable moments. Now we're Hotel Lismore. The name has changed, but the essence hasn't: we're still the local pub you've known and loved, serving Australian pub cuisine, live sports and family-friendly entertainment.

Settle into our cosy lounges or the leafy beer garden, tuck into a daily special, catch the big match with fellow fans, or test your wits at trivia. Plans for refurbishment and expansion are well underway, so stay tuned. Thank you for being part of our journey. From Catherine, Ashleigh and the Hotel Lismore Team.

Functions and Events

Bring your people together at Hotel Lismore. We have flexible spaces, generous food and a team that makes planning straightforward from the first enquiry to the last round. Birthdays, engagement parties, work lunches, team events or cocktail evenings: we'll shape the space, food and drinks around your guest list, style and budget.

  • Casual pub gatherings, family celebrations and milestone events
  • Spaces for small groups through to events of up to 200 guests
  • Sharing platters, wood-fired pizza platters and three set-menu options
  • Public Bar settings plus the leafy Garden Bar and semi-alfresco Terrace

You can enjoy a comfortable meal in the Public Bar, settle into the Cocktail Lounge, or use the central Courtyard as an open-air gathering point between the main areas of the hotel. The Public Bar is a classic pub setting for relaxed celebrations, work lunches and casual group gatherings.
