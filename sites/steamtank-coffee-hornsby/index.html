About

SteamTank Coffee Hornsby is at 2 Station St, pouring coffee that even home baristas come back for. The coffee is from Legend roaster, made on a La Marzocco Strada EP with Anfim SP2 and Mahlkönig EK43 grinders. Order it as espresso or batch brew.

Dine in or grab it to take away, with vegetarian options and a bacon and egg roll that regulars keep recommending. You can also take home beans from Mecca, Small Batch and Legend.
