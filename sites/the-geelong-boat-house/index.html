The Geelong Boat House is a refurbished vintage barge on the Western Foreshore, overlooking Corio Bay. We serve Modern Australian seafood and classic fish and chips, with a deck for sunny days and wood heaters for cooler ones.

Come up the ramp, place your order and pay, and we'll call you when it's ready to collect. Dine in, sit outside on the deck or take it away for a picnic on the waterfront. Bookings are welcome.
