Thai Thae opened its first doors in Hurstville in 2016 with a simple dream: to bring the authentic taste and warm spirit of Thailand to Sydney. We started with just 40 seats and a kitchen full of passion, cooking the dishes we grew up loving.

Today you'll find us on the rooftop at Westfield Hurstville, licensed and BYO, with outdoor seating and vegetarian options. Dine in with family, grab a quick lunch, or order takeaway or delivery for a cosy night in.

Our Story

Recipes passed down through generations

Every dish at Thai Thae tells a story. Our Massamun Curry is slow-cooked overnight to develop deep, complex flavours, and our whole Barramundi is prepared six different ways, each celebrating a different region of Thailand. We believe great food should be accessible to all, from humble street food gems to extravagant delights.

Growing across Sydney

Word spread fast. What began as a humble kitchen in Hurstville has grown into nine restaurants across Sydney: the rooftop bar at Westfield Hurstville, Mascot, Randwick, Penrith, Lidcombe, Eastgardens, Melrose Park and Marrickville Metro, with Box Hill to follow in 2027. Each location carries the same heart, the same recipes and the same commitment to authentic Thai flavours.

More than just food

Thai Thae is a celebration of Thai culture, hospitality and the belief that sharing a meal brings people together. We want every bite to transport you to the streets of Thailand.
