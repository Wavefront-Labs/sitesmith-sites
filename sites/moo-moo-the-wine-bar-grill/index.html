For over two decades, Moo Moo the Wine Bar + Grill at Broadbeach has been a cornerstone of family dining on the Gold Coast, renowned for its unparalleled beef offerings. Its reputation for delivering flawlessly crafted steaks remains unmatched. At Moo Moo, our beef dishes take centre stage, showcasing a diverse selection of premium cuts from both local and international sources. Some cuts are exclusive to Moo Moo, making it the ultimate destination for those who value exceptional quality and flavour in family dining Gold Coast .

Our Awards are a Reflection of Our

Commitment to Excellence

Since opening the doors in June 2005 with Moo Moo Restaurant Group founders, Steven and Autumn Adams, Moo Moo The Wine Bar + Grill Gold Coast quickly found its following, and the awards began to flow.

In its first year of full operation, Moo Moo was awarded the 2006 Gold Coast Best New Restaurant. From there, the awards continued. In 2023, Moo Moo was awarded Gold Coast Best Steak Restaurant by the Restaurant Industry body, Restaurant and Catering Australia.

Check out our Awards page to see some of our recent awards.

Moo Moo Restaurant

where steak dreams come true

Over the years, Steven has built up excellent knowledge and relationships within the Australian Beef Industry, which provide us with an exceptionally diverse, award-winning, beef menu of the highest quality, with some cuts exclusive to the Moo Moo Restaurant Group . These cuts are handpicked by Steven and include: Dry Aged, Pasture Fed, All Natural and Grain Fed, which include a mix of Wagyu and Angus breeds.

As one of the standout Gold Coast lunch restaurants , Moo Moo Restaurant Group Executive Chef, Trent Robson, works alongside quality boutique suppliers and oversees Gold Coast’s Head Chef, Tom Minter, delivering menus that celebrate fresh seasonal flavours to ensure that our menus cater for more than just beef lovers; they also include fresh seafood, poultry, game and of course, vegetarian options available.

Our Moobar Has Become a

Favourite Meeting Place

To complement the restaurant, the accompanying Moobar has become one of the Gold Coast’s hot spots. Through the week, the bar is teeming with Gold Coast locals and the many tourists and convention attendees who enjoy the amazing cocktails, mocktails and a multiple award-winning wine list recognised by Gourmet Traveller and Australia’s Wine List of the Year Awards. Moobar is also a popular birthday party venue for Gold Coast residents for memorable celebrations.

Moo Moo The Wine Bar + Grill:

Experience Steak, like never before!

Since opening our doors on the 4th of June 2010, Moo Moo The Wine Bar + Grill Brisbane has redefined the city's culinary landscape. Located in the stunning, historical Port Office Building in the heart of the Brisbane CBD, our restaurant is a sophisticated sanctuary for meat lovers and wine connoisseurs alike.

Situated near the Eagle Street Pier, the lush Botanical Gardens, and the prestigious Stamford Plaza complex, we have quickly evolved into a local icon, widely regarded as the best steak restaurant Brisbane boasts and one of the premier steak houses in Australia.

A World-Class Steakhouse

Experience in Brisbane

At Moo Moo, we don't just serve exceptional steak – we celebrate the craft behind it. As one of Brisbane's leading steakhouses, our menu showcases an expertly curated selection of premium beef sourced from some of Australia's most respected producers and renowned international Wagyu specialists.

While Moo Moo is renowned as a premier Brisbane steakhouse, our menu also features a sophisticated selection of fresh seafood and expertly prepared poultry, delivering a truly memorable fine dining experience.
