Since June 2005, Moo Moo The Wine Bar + Grill has been serving some of the Gold Coast's finest steaks in the heart of Broadbeach. We overlook Victoria Park, opposite the Gold Coast Sofitel, The Star Gold Coast and the Gold Coast Convention and Exhibition Centre.

Beef is the star here, with 20 cuts available at any time and some exclusive to Moo Moo. There's also fresh seafood, poultry, game and vegetarian options, an award-winning list of over 900 wines, and our Moobar for a drink before dinner or a whole evening out.

Experience Steak, Like Never Before

For more than two decades, Moo Moo has been a cornerstone of family dining on the Gold Coast. Our cuts come from local and international sources, from pasture-fed beef to ultra-marbled Wagyu imported from Japan.

Long partnerships with top producers give us a steady supply of exclusive cuts and high marble scores. Unlike restaurants that often switch suppliers, we can keep the quality consistent from one visit to the next.

Why choose Moo Moo

  • Exceptional beef from world-class producers
  • Exclusive partnerships with breeders and suppliers
  • One of the most extensive steak and wine menus in Australia
  • Cuts and marble scores allocated exclusively to Moo Moo
  • Fresh seafood, expertly prepared poultry and premium game too

Our Story

Moo Moo Restaurant Group founders Steven and Autumn Adams opened the doors in June 2005. In our first full year we were named 2006 Gold Coast Best New Restaurant. In 2023, Restaurant and Catering Australia awarded us Gold Coast Best Steak Restaurant.

Over the years Steven has built strong knowledge of and relationships within the Australian beef industry. He handpicks our cuts, including Dry Aged, Pasture Fed, All Natural and Grain Fed, across both Wagyu and Angus breeds.

Group Executive Chef Trent Robson works with quality boutique suppliers and oversees Gold Coast Head Chef Tom Minter. Together they create menus that celebrate fresh seasonal flavours for beef lovers and everyone else.

The Restaurant

Relax in our modern, open-plan dining room. For 20 years our team has refined the recipe for fantastic food, great wine and exceptional service.

Our menus tell you the breed, age, size and diet of every cut. Our Specialty Beef includes some of the nation's finest, including Master Kobe Beef, the richest and most luxurious in Australia. Fresh, locally sourced produce rounds out the beef, seafood and poultry.

Moobar

Everybody is welcome at Broadbeach's favourite wine bar. Locals, holidaymakers and conference delegates all drop in, whether for a pre-dinner drink, a leisurely lunch over a glass of wine or a whole evening out. Grab an alfresco high-top table and watch the sun set over Victoria Park with a cocktail.

Choose from over 900 wines on a list recognised by Gourmet Traveller and Australia's Wine List of the Year Awards. There are also cocktails crafted by expert mixologists, mocktails and international premium spirits. Beer drinkers can pick from local, imported and craft beers, including our own Moo Moo Angus Amber Organic Ale.

Functions at Moobar

Moobar suits everything from small groups with table reservations to canapé and wine functions for up to 300 guests, with canapés and drink packages on offer. Call our functions team on (+61) 7 5539 9952.

Events and Functions

There's something on all year at Moo Moo, from the Melbourne Cup to New Year's celebrations. We can also help you organise your own charity event or corporate function.

Our open-plan layout seats up to 140 guests, making it ideal for weddings, charity events, corporate dinners, product launches and more. Moobar suits cocktail parties for small groups up to 80 guests, and the entire venue holds up to 250 guests cocktail style.
