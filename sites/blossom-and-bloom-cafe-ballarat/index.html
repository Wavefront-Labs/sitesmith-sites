Blossom & Bloom is a cafe at 202 Humffray St S in Bakery Hill, Ballarat, serving brunch, coffee and dessert. Guests talk about fresh, flavour-packed food that's beautifully presented, rich and smooth coffee, and friendly staff in a lovely, welcoming space.

Come in to dine, grab something to take away, or book a table ahead online through OpenTable.
