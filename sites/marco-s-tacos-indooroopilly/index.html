Marco's Tacos is a Mexican restaurant at Shop 1/172 Clarence Rd, Indooroopilly. Come in for a sit-down meal, grab takeaway or have it delivered, and there are vegetarian options on offer. Bookings are taken.

Locals tell us they come back for the fajitas, the hand cut house-made corn chips and the spicy margs. Finish with a scoop from the gelato bar in the front window. It's a relaxed spot for a family meal out, and even on a packed Friday night the kitchen keeps things moving.
