The Dak is a takeaway spot at 21 Nind St, Southport, known for generous portions of Korean fried chicken with a thin, crispy batter and big, juicy pieces. Our bulgogi burger, on a soft bun with pickles, is a staff favourite.

Pick up your order, get it delivered, or grab a seat outside. Our regulars come back every week, and we'd love you to become one of them.
