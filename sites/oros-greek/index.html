Oros Greek is a little Greek corner in Chermside West, cooking authentic Greek food without trying too hard. You taste olive oil, herbs, grilled meat and simple things done right, on a menu that sticks close to tradition but still feels current.

Tables fill with families and friends talking over shared plates, and the place has that lived-in feel. Dine in, book a table, grab takeaway or have it delivered. If you're after a quick yiros fix, try the wraps.

Our Story

Oros Greek was born from a love of simple, honest cooking: dishes made with care, tradition and the flavours of home. What started as a passion for family recipes has grown into a modern Greek dining experience, blending authentic heritage with fresh, bold tastes.

Ingredients that matter

Greek cuisine is built on balance, generosity and simplicity. From extra-virgin olive oil and fresh herbs to vibrant vegetables, tender meats and coastal seafood, we let real ingredients shine. We've kept our menu focused so we can give every dish extra care and serve you quicker, with more dishes coming soon.

Our promise

To cook with passion. To serve with love. To create moments worth remembering. Whether you're celebrating with loved ones or enjoying a quiet meal, every visit is a small getaway to Greece.
