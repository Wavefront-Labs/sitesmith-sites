Kinnaree Thai Restaurant serves Thai food at Shop 1B/37 Benabrow Ave, Bellara. Dine in with us, take your meal home, or have it delivered. Vegetarian options are available, and bookings are welcome on 3408 6555.

Our guests keep coming back for the papaya salad, sea bass, calamari, coconut prawns, fried rice and the chef's special pork belly with noodles. Ask for extra spicy and we'll happily turn up the heat.
