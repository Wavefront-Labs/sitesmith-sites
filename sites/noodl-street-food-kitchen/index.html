Noodl Street Food Kitchen serves casual Asian fusion from the lower ground floor at 38 Mann Street, Gosford. Eat in, sit outside, or order online for pick-up.

Locals keep coming back for the corn ribs, the pork wontons with house-made chilli oil, the chashu pork ramen and the melt-in-your-mouth pork bao. Add a watermelon lychee spritz and grab a seat outside. The team is cheerful and quick, even in the lunch rush.
