OUR STORY

HISTORY

It’s hard to imagine living in a major Australian city and literally having no place to go for a cup of specialty coffee. When our founder Martin Richards returned from NZ back in 2006, this was the scenario he faced.

He’d been roasting for Peoples Coffee in Wellington and had developed a taste for specialty coffee. It was clear what needed to be done. Inauspiciously (but quite romantically) we started roasting in the old footy sheds at Souths Leagues Club, under the Moreton Bay figs. Our Probat L12 roasting machine arrived in May 2007 and by July it was calibrated and we started roasting for our very first wholesale coffee customer: Jamies Espresso Bar, Fortitude Valley.

In 2008, we opened our original store in a back street of inner city West End. With strong community networks, a following amongst students and creatives, and a presence selling beans and cold press coffee down at the Davies Park Markets, our early pioneers established a Brisbane institution. One that continues to mean a lot to many locals.

PRESENT DAY

Fast forward to 2020 and Blackstar is now 13 years old. With 16 plus million brews under our belts, we’ve paid our dues as a heritage coffee brand and are still passionate about our mission, bringing specialty coffee to life. We now roast and distribute from our roastery in East Brisbane, which is a purpose-built centre for all things coffee roasting, cupping, barista training, cold brewing, coffee events and more. You can make changes to your subscription by logging into your account at any time, including frequency, size, coffee selection or grind type. Also Skip and Reschedule orders.

CHANGES TO AN ORDER

I made a mistake on my order what do I do? Provided the order has not yet been dispatched, we can make a change for you. Please, preferably, email us at roastery@blackstarcoffee.com.au or call 07 3217 2323 - we try to answer calls quickly however roasting machines are loud and if we are roasting we may have to call you back, please leave a detailed message, thanks for understanding.

TRACKING YOUR ORDER

If you are looking at tracking your order, keep an eye for the tracking email that gets sent when the order is dispatched, which links to tracking on Shippit.

LATE ORDERS

We have reached out to Australia Post, Couriers Please and Aramex for shipping; currently the delays can occur due to several reasons such as increased volumes and eventual sorting facilities problems. Some delays are unavoidable but if your order is more than 3 days past expected delivery please email roastery@blackstarcoffee.com.au so we can assist you.

RETURNS

To assist our team in providing you with a speedy resolution, please include photos and/or a short video demonstrating the damage or fault with your product. Email information to roastery@blackstarcoffee.com.au

Roasting great coffee, and drinking it, gets us out of bed in the morning at Blackstar. We know that the cost of coffee production is much more than monetary, with land clearing and unsustainable farming practices, such as mono-cropping, in overseas regions. OUR ETHOS

VISION / MISSION / VALUES

Blackstar’s Vision is to stimulate the transition to sustainability . T o caffeinate the movement towards a sustainable economy. We understand our business to be a vehicle for change and we believe that great coffee can change the world.

Our goal is to create positive impact through the culture we are creating. Operationally we are moving towards more sustainable business practices. From choice of suppliers, to composting our coffee waste and promoting re-use cups. We also aim to join with others in this movement, to inspire hope and to create a spark. This is the ‘stimulate’ aspect, much more than profiteering.

‘Transition’ refers to the journey and accepting that change is a process. We recognise that just because we can’t do everything now, doesn’t mean we can’t do something now. As long as we’re on the way and moving as best we can.

Lastly, “sustainability”. The much over-used and under-acted upon S-word. We interpret it holistically and based on the five capitals of sustainable development: personal, social, physical, environmental and financial. These five dimensions cover all aspects of the kind of sustainability we are working towards in our business, in our own lives, and in the world around us.

TRADE JUSTICE

We buy green coffee in alignment with the following non-negotiable's, as outlined in our Ethical Procurement Policy:

A. Integrity, ethics and fair conduct throughout the supply chain

B. Traceability through a demonstrated direct relationship with farmers

C. Fair price to coffee producers.

D. Ethical labour and human rights practices throughout the chain of supply such as gender equality practices

E. Fair wages and working conditions for farm labourers.

F. Environmental management and sustainable practices.

We screen our suppliers to ensure that they operate in accordance with these standards.

Where possible we purchase directly from the coffee producers themselves. And where this isn’t the case we commit to only buy from importers that have a direct relationship with the farmers and co-operative’s. We also place high value on community, environmental and specialty coffee projects funded and supported by our suppliers.

We’re very proud to be partnered with the following amazing green coffee suppliers, each of whom are very committed to the wellbeing of the growers they are partnered with. Several of our import partners are from coffee producing families themselves and have settled in a Australia to help make a future for their communities.
