Our Story

It's hard to imagine living in a major Australian city with nowhere to get a cup of specialty coffee. That was the situation our founder Martin Richards found when he returned from NZ in 2006. He'd been roasting for Peoples Coffee in Wellington and had developed a taste for specialty coffee, so it was clear what needed to be done.

Inauspiciously (but quite romantically), we started roasting in the old footy sheds at Souths Leagues Club, under the Moreton Bay figs. Our Probat L12 roasting machine arrived in May 2007, and by July it was calibrated and roasting for our very first wholesale customer, Jamies Espresso Bar in Fortitude Valley. In 2008 we opened our original store in West End.

Present day

By 2020 Blackstar was 13 years old, with more than 16 million brews behind us and still passionate about our mission: bringing specialty coffee to life. We now roast and distribute from our purpose-built roastery in East Brisbane, home to roasting, cupping, barista training, cold brewing and coffee events. We're committed to fair pricing for farmers, specialty grade beans, supporting young baristas and educating customers, and many of our staff have gone on to do great things in the industry.

Our Ethos

Our vision is to stimulate the transition to sustainability, to caffeinate the movement towards a sustainable economy. We see our business as a vehicle for change, and we believe great coffee can change the world. Day to day, that means choosing suppliers carefully, composting our coffee waste and promoting re-use cups.

We accept that change is a process. Just because we can't do everything now doesn't mean we can't do something now. We think about sustainability holistically, across five capitals: personal, social, physical, environmental and financial.

Trade justice

Our Ethical Procurement Policy sets out our non-negotiables for buying green coffee:

  • Integrity, ethics and fair conduct throughout the supply chain
  • Traceability through a demonstrated direct relationship with farmers
  • Fair price to coffee producers
  • Ethical labour and human rights practices throughout the chain of supply, such as gender equality practices
  • Fair wages and working conditions for farm labourers
  • Environmental management and sustainable practices

Where possible we buy directly from coffee producers. Otherwise we only buy from importers with a direct relationship with farmers and co-operatives. We're proud to partner with Project Origin Coffee, Cofinet and 3Brothers. Several of our import partners come from coffee-producing families themselves and have settled in Australia to help build a future for their communities.

Coffee For Trees

Drink coffee, plant trees. We know coffee production costs more than money, through land clearing and unsustainable practices like mono-cropping overseas. East coast Australia also has one of the highest rates of land clearing in the world. Through our Coffee For Trees initiative, we're doing what we can to build a coffee economy that enhances our communities and our planet.
