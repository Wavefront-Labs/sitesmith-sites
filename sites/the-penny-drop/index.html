The Penny Drop is a vibrant all-day eatery on Whitehorse Road, serving breakfast, lunch and dinner from a modern Australian menu. Expect everything from breakfast classics through to unique share plates with an Asian-inspired twist, alongside coffee our regulars rave about.

We're a 100-seater restaurant on the ground floor of the newly refurbished ATO building. Designed by We Are Huntly, the space combines high ceilings and muted earthy tones to create a warm and inviting oasis, perfect for escaping the daily Melbourne grind.

Our Story

The Penny Drop began as a little coffee pop-up in Box Hill, where hundreds of bleary-eyed commuters dropped by every morning for their freshly prepared coffees. Seeing their enthusiasm, and realising Box Hill was missing a vibrant all-day eatery, the penny metaphorically dropped for the owners.

Today we're a permanent home for good food and coffee, fully licensed and open seven days a week. We've been featured in some of Melbourne's most loved publications, including Good Food Guide, Broadsheet, Urban List and Weekend Notes.
