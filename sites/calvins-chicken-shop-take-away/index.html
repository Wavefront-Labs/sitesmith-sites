About

  • Takeaway
  • Delivery

Calvins Chicken Shop & Take-away on Gordon Street, Port Macquarie, serves casual meals built around freshly cooked chicken. Whether you're after a quick bite, a family dinner or catering for a group, we've got you covered.

Alongside our BBQ chooks you'll find fresh salads, burgers, steak sandwiches and a good variety of made-to-order hot food. Eat in, take it home or have it delivered, with wheelchair access throughout. We're here for locals and visitors alike.
