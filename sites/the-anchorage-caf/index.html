Berthing and storage

Akuna Bay Marina is on Coal and Candle Creek within Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park, north of Sydney. Its sheltered setting connects with Cowan Creek and the wider Hawkesbury River system, with Pittwater accessible via Broken Bay.

The marina offers 204 wet berths for vessels up to 40 metres, 167 covered dry stack spaces and swing moorings. Casual and longer-term options are subject to complete vessel dimensions, suitability and current availability.

Suitable boats are professionally lifted, retrieved and stored in a secure covered rack. Launch procedures, vessel limits, notice periods and any included wash-down services should be confirmed with the Akuna Bay team.

Marina services and visiting

Facilities include showers, toilets, laundry and a guest lounge. Shed Kiosk provides casual waterfront food and coffee. Check current opening hours directly before visiting.

Exploring the local area

From Akuna Bay, boaters can explore the sheltered reaches of Cowan Creek and the Hawkesbury River system. Longer trips towards Pittwater and Broken Bay depend on vessel capability, weather and sea conditions, so use current charts and marine forecasts.

Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park surrounds the marina and offers bushwalks, lookouts, picnic areas and Aboriginal cultural sites. Access conditions can change, so check current NSW National Parks information and remember that national park rules apply throughout the precinct.
