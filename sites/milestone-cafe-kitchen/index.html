Milestone Cafe & Kitchen is a cosy cafe right in the centre of Southport, at c001/56 Scarborough St. Guests come for the good variety of food, quick service and carefully made coffee and cold brew.

Dine in, grab takeaway or take a seat outside. There are vegetarian options too. We're a small space, so at busy times it's worth popping by a little early to snag a table.
