Nowhere Espresso Pizzeria and Wine Bar is Toowong's local cafe and Italian restaurant, tucked away at 8 Bent St. We're open for breakfast, lunch and dinner, and proudly serve Bear Bones Coffee.

Locals call us a slightly hidden gem, with good tunes, friendly dogs and a warm welcome. Pull up a seat inside or out, or take it with you.
