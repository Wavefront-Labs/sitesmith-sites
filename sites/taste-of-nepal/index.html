The story of Taste of Nepal is simple. What was, still is: an undying passion for food, a strong belief in the experience of dining and a team driven by love.

We started Adelaide's first Nepalese restaurant and spread the warmth of Nepalese food in this quaint city. For us, food is about being innovative and authentic: freshly ground spices and the art of blending them into new dishes. Our kitchen is led by Head Chef Nil.
