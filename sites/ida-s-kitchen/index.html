Ida's Kitchen is a cafe at 250 Olsen Ave, Parkwood, in the small shopping village across from Southport Sharks. It's surprisingly spacious inside, with a calm, relaxed atmosphere and a good menu with plenty to choose from, including vegetarian options.

Guests often mention the kind, welcoming staff and food that's fresh and full of flavour, from French toast to bánh mì and beef pho. Pop in to dine in, grab takeaway, order delivery or make a booking. We're rated 4.7 from 231 reviews.
