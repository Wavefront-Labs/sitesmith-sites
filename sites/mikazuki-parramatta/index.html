Mikazuki Parramatta is a Japanese restaurant at Shop 5/169 Macquarie St, Parramatta, known for its ramen and fresh sashimi. Come in to dine, grab takeaway or make a booking. There are vegetarian options too.

Locals have rated us 4.4 from 2,337 Google reviews, and many keep coming back for lunch and dinner.
