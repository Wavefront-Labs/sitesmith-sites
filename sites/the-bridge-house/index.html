The Bridge House is a restaurant and wine bar at 1 Harvest Rd, North Fremantle. Guests talk about the cosy, low-key feel, the beautiful fit-out and friendly, relaxed service. On a cold, wet day the fire is roaring.

Come in for brunch, a long lunch or a dinner date once it's dark outside. We have outdoor seating and vegetarian options, and you can book a table online at bridgehousefreo.com.au/booking.html.

Functions and Private Dining

We have a space for every kind of get-together, from a quiet business meeting to a birthday or an intimate wedding reception.

Our sun-dappled main space is ideal for long lunches and is beautifully lit in the evening.

Private Dining Room

An intimate space for celebrating special occasions. Also suitable for private meetings.

Back Dining

This light and airy room is perfect for casual family dining, and it's a cool reprieve during Perth's hot summer.

Courtyard

A cosy spot for group dining. It's undercover and heated, so it suits winter. It's perfect for small gatherings such as intimate wedding receptions and birthday celebrations.
