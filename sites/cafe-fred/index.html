Cafe Fred has been part of High Street in Penrith for years, and our regulars will tell you it's well worth the visit. Drop in for brekkie and a coffee, grab something to go, or have it delivered. Locals tell us they love the reasonable prices, the excellent food and the super friendly staff.

Our space is interesting and cultured, a little cosy, and somehow it just works. Vegetarian options are on the menu, and we're happy to look after your dietary needs. Come and see why we're rated 4.5 stars from over 200 reviews.
