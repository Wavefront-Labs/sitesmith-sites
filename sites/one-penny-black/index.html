The One Penny Black experience is simple. Honest. Crafted. Quality. Coffee is at the heart of what we do, and it's our pleasure to share one of life's finest fruits with you, from origin to roaster, roaster to cup.

Find us at 196 Hunter Street in Newcastle's east end. We're sympathetically lit, laid back and comfortable, with a homely warmth in any season. Join us for dine in, takeaway, delivery or a table outside.

Coffee

With knowledge comes understanding. With passion comes love in your cup.

We proudly serve a custom roasted 100% Arabica House Blend, crafted exclusively for us with tasting notes of milk chocolate, dulce de leche and creamy hazelnut.

We rotate carefully selected single origins from Colombia, Ethiopia, Guji & Papua New Guinea. Alongside them we pour weekly guest single origins, washed filter coffees and decaf from ST. ALi.

How we brew

  • Espresso
  • Single Origin Espresso
  • V60 Pour Over
  • Batch Brew
  • Cold Brew
  • Cold Drip

Everything is brewed on the iconic Synesso Cyncra. Retail 1kg bean bags are available in-store.

Food

One Penny grub is unique and wholesome, letting locally sourced produce speak for itself.

Our menu is seasonal, with classics that never change and consideration for diverse diets, including vegetarian options.

Ambience
