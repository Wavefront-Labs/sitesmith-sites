The Caffeine Garden is a rooftop cafe in Pavilion D on Level 4 of Westfield Hurstville, right next to Event Cinema. You can dine in, sit outside, grab takeaway, order delivery or book a table.

We're rated 4.6 from 328 Google reviews, and guests keep coming back to the coffee: rich, smooth and finished with beautiful latte art. Reviewers also mention fresh, generous food, from ham croissants and breakfast wraps to burgers and steak, served by a team that takes real pride in looking after every customer.
