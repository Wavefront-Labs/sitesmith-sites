About

Pelicano Espresso is a small coffee shop at 371 Marine Parade, Labrador, right on the Broadwater walk. Grab a cappuccino or an iced mocha to take with you, or stay a while and watch the seagulls.

Alongside the coffee you'll find acai smoothies, a selection of baked treats like friands and chocolate cookies, and a small ice cream bar for the hot days. Whether you dine in or take away, expect a warm welcome.
