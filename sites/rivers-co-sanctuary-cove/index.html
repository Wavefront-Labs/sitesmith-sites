Rivers & Co. A Place You'll Always Feel Welcome

Rivers & Co. is a hospitality brand built on more than 30 years of creating memorable dining experiences. What began in Melbourne has grown into Queensland, where our passion for exceptional food, genuine hospitality and premium casual dining continues to bring people together.

Our first Rivers & Co. destination is now open at the iconic Sanctuary Cove Marina on the Gold Coast. Formerly the much-loved Red's Kitchen & Bar, it now begins a new chapter as Rivers & Co., serving premium steaks, fresh local seafood and relaxed waterfront dining in one of Australia's most beautiful marina settings.

This is just the beginning of the Rivers & Co. story. In the months ahead, we'll continue to welcome new destinations into the Rivers & Co. family, each sharing our commitment to exceptional food, warm hospitality and unforgettable experiences.

We believe that great dining is built on three things: quality, consistency and genuine hospitality.

Our menus are driven by premium ingredients and thoughtful preparation. Our spaces are designed to feel refined yet welcoming. Our service is warm, attentive and always professional.

From perfectly prepared steaks to expertly executed seafood, every dish reflects precision, skill and care.

Thoughtfully curated details, from the plate to the atmosphere, creating a polished dining experience.

Across every Rivers & Co. venue, guests can expect the same elevated standard... every visit, every time.

Seasonal, premium ingredients sourced with intention to deliver depth, freshness and flavour.

Warm, attentive service that makes every guest feel genuinely welcomed, valued and at ease.

More than dining, each visit is designed to feel considered, memorable and worth returning for.

A Place You'll Always Feel Welcome

At Rivers & Co., we believe great hospitality is about making people feel at home.

From the moment you arrive, you'll be welcomed with genuine warmth, thoughtful service and a relaxed atmosphere where every guest feels valued.

Whether you're celebrating a special occasion, catching up over a long lunch or simply enjoying dinner with family and friends, we want every visit to feel effortless, memorable and worth coming back for. It's this commitment to genuine hospitality that turns first-time guests into familiar faces.

We can accommodate the following dietary requirements - gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian and vegan. Unfortunately we cannot cater to allergies or diseases due to supplier advise that they are unable to guarantee that cross contamination has not occurred. Your safety is our priority and for this reason, we cannot cater to allergies or diseases.
