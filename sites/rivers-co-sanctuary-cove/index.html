Rivers & Co. is a hospitality brand built on more than 30 years of creating memorable dining experiences. What began in Melbourne has grown into Queensland, bringing exceptional food, genuine hospitality and premium casual dining to the iconic Sanctuary Cove Marina on the Gold Coast.

Formerly the much-loved Red's Kitchen & Bar, our venue now begins a new chapter as Rivers & Co., serving premium steaks, fresh local seafood and relaxed waterfront dining. Whether you're celebrating a special occasion, catching up over a long lunch or simply enjoying dinner with family and friends, we want every visit to feel effortless, memorable and worth coming back for.

Our Approach

We believe great dining is built on three things: quality, consistency and genuine hospitality. Our menus are driven by seasonal, premium ingredients and thoughtful preparation, from perfectly prepared steaks to expertly executed seafood.

Our spaces are designed to feel refined yet welcoming, and our service is warm, attentive and always professional. It's this commitment to genuine hospitality that turns first-time guests into familiar faces.

Bookings

Book online or call us on 07 5510 9988.

  • Reservations are held for a maximum of 15 minutes. If you are running late, please call us. If your table is booked after you, we may need to reduce your allocated time.
  • We can accommodate gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian and vegan diets. We cannot cater to allergies or diseases, as our suppliers are unable to guarantee that cross contamination has not occurred.
  • Bookings of 4 guests or more require a credit card guarantee. We won't charge your card, but if you do not arrive or cancel within 24 hours we will charge $50 per guest.
