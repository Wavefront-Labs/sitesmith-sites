The Cube Hotel is a steakhouse in the heart of Toowoomba, on the corner of Margaret and Neil Streets, serving lunch and dinner 7 days a week until late. It's home to Stonegrill dining, exclusive to The Cube Hotel, where your steak or seafood arrives at the table still cooking on a hot stone so you control the cook.

Spread over two storeys with four bar areas and a wraparound verandah, it's all-day dining, so bring your friends and family for a day and night to remember. And it's not just steaks we're famous for: our dessert creations are the perfect end to a perfect meal.

Stonegrill Dining

Stonegrill is an exciting concept that lets you take control of the cooking process. Simply choose from our menu and your fresh ingredients are served to your table still cooking on super-heated volcanic stones.

The stones are specially selected for their heat retention and baked to 400 degrees in our purpose-built Stonegrill oven. Meals are presented in a protective wooden serving tray with crispy chips, aioli and house slaw.

Our dedicated chefs use fresh local and seasonal ingredients to give you the hottest flavour in town.

In-house Dry Aged Steak

A little aged never hurt anyone. New to the Cube Hotel Steakhouse is our in-house dry aged steak. Dry ageing is a natural process that intensifies a steak's flavour while making it very tender, for a wonderfully rich taste experience.
