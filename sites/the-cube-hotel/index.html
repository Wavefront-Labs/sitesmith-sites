STEAKHOUSE

What is Stonegrill Dining?

It's an exciting concept that let's you take control of the cooking process!

The Stonegrill Concept is exclusive to The Cube Hotel.

Simply select from our menu and the fresh ingredients are served to your table still cooking on super-heated volcanic stones.

These specially selected stones, chosen for their heat retention, are baked to 400 degrees in our purpose-built Stonegrill oven.

Meals are presented within a protective wooden serving tray, sided by crispy chips, aioli, and house slaw

Using fresh local and seasonal ingredients, our dedicated chefs look forward to giving you a taste sensation that is the hottest flavour in town!

A little aged never hurt anyone! New to the Cube Hotel Steakhouse is our In-house Dry Aged steak. Drying aging is a natural process that intensifies a steak’s flavour, t the same time creating a very tender steak. Tender and tasty! Giving you a wonderfully rich taste experience.
