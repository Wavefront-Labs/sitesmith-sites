Olive Coffee is a friendly cafe at Shop 3/3046 Surfers Paradise Blvd. Guests mention the warm, welcoming feel and genuinely friendly service, and it's rated 4.8 from 578 Google reviews.

Come in for good coffee and a bite to eat. Regulars and visitors rave about the fresh, flaky almond croissant, the juicy peri peri chicken and the Italian sandwiches. Dine in, grab takeaway or sit outside, with vegetarian options available.
