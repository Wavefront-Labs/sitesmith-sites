Welcome to Treaty of Paris, where French technique meets Australian soul. Our story is simple: a love for the craft of pastry and the joy of sharing it with our community. Every croissant, tart and loaf of sourdough is made with care using classic French methods and the finest Australian ingredients.

This is our treaty: a promise to keep things authentic, affordable and made fresh every day. Whether you're here for your morning coffee, a weekend brunch or something sweet to take home, we hope every visit feels like a little escape, a moment where time slows and life tastes good.

Menu

Made Fresh Every Day

From our 72-hour fermented sourdough to our handmade pastries and desserts, each creation is a celebration of balance: the elegance of France, the freshness of Australia and the warmth of Sandgate.

Find us at 104 Wakefield St, Sandgate, just up from the water, with a covered outdoor area for coffee in the sea breeze. Dine in, take away, or book ahead online.
