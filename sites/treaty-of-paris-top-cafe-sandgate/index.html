Welcome to Treaty of Paris , where French technique meets Australian soul . Our story is simple — a love for the craft of pastry and the joy of sharing it with our community. Every croissant, tart, and loaf of sourdough is made with care using classic French methods and the finest Australian ingredients . This is our treaty : a promise to keep things authentic, affordable, and made fresh every day .
