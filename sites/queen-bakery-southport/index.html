Queen Bakery is a cosy little Vietnamese restaurant tucked away at Shop 4/129 Queen St, Southport. Pull up a seat and dine in, grab your order to take away, or have it delivered.

With a 4.9 rating from 70 Google reviews, it's well worth the trip. Guests rave about the banh mi, the generous portions and the friendly staff who go out of their way to look after you. The pho is a favourite too, with reviewers praising its rich broth, cooked for 12 hours.
