Shiny Brew is a cafe at Unit 3/62 Cylinders Dr, Kingscliff, serving breakfast, coffee and smoothies. Eat in, grab it to take away, or settle in at one of the outdoor tables. There are vegetarian options too.

Plenty of people staying in Kingscliff for a weekend away or a wedding find us one morning and come back the next. Big groups are looked after as well. We've pushed tables together to fit 14 for breakfast. Shiny Brew is rated 4.5 from 248 Google reviews.
