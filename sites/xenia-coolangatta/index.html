Xenia is the ancient Greek concept of hospitality: the generosity and courtesy shown to those who are far from home. We're a family-owned Greek restaurant inspired by Kastellorizo Island, and we're passionate about genuine Greek hospitality. Experience a slice of Greece with every visit.

Our menu is traditional Greek food, with local and Greek wines and beers on tap. Pull up a chair inside or outside, book a table, or take Xenia home. We look forward to seeing you soon.

What's On

Bouzoukia Sundays

Live bouzouki, smashing plates, long lunches, dancing and pure Greek energy. Bouzoukia Sundays are back at Xenia, bringing the spirit of Greece to the Gold Coast each month.

Tuesday Skewers

Greek skewers, pita and tzatziki for $22 every Tuesday from 5pm. Casual, share-style dining done the Xenia way.

Thursday Locals Night

It's the locals' event of the week, every Thursday night. Enjoy a special Chef's Banquet with many Xenia favourite dishes, and Happy Hour all night long.

New Year's Eve

Celebrate New Year's Eve with a Greek banquet, live music, dancing, plate smashing and plenty of OPA. Want to celebrate early? Join us for a Greek family feast from 4pm to 5.45pm, perfect for little ones. This seating is separate from our main NYE celebration. Menu and pricing coming soon.

Functions and Events

Celebrate the Greek way at Xenia. We host milestone birthdays, engagement parties, long lunches, corporate functions, weddings and private events, with private dining and full venue hire available. Our food and drinks packages can be tailored to your taste and budget.

Our Values

  • Philoxenia: the Greek word for welcoming even the stranger into your world. When we open our restaurant, we open our hearts and welcome you in.
  • Meraki: the essence of yourself that you put into your work, your soul, creativity and passion. It's the ingredient that sets Xenia apart.
  • Play like Leonidas: lead by example, take ownership, and let actions speak louder than words.
  • Be like Aristotle: be resourceful, insightful and innovative, and solve challenges creatively.
  • Don't be a Malaka: our no-nonsense rule. Talk straight, be honest, and build teamwork.
