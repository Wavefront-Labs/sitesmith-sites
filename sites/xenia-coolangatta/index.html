YIASOU

Welcome to Xenia Bar & Dining Restaurant Gold Coast

Xenia is the ancient Greek concept of hospitality, the generosity and courtesy shown to those who are far from home. A family-owned Greek restaurant,

Xenia is truly passionate about genuine Greek hospitality and the best restaurant across Coolangatta and Main Beach Gold Coast. Experience a slice of Greece with every visit, and we look forward to seeing you soon.

OUR VALUES

Play like Leonidas

Lead by example. Take ownership. Inspire those around you and work alongside your colleagues, in a mission to succeed. Actions speak louder than your words.

Meraki

This is the essence of yourself that you put into your work – your soul, creativity, and passion. The ingredient we have that sets Xenia apart.

Philoxenia

Gathering with others around our table is something we treasure and is high on our core values Philoxenia - the Greek word for welcoming even the stranger into your world. When we open our restaurant, we open our hearts and welcome you in and give you an authentic Greek heartfelt hospitality experience.

Be like Aristotle

Display prudence, temperance, courage, and justice; Be resourceful, insightful, innovative. Think outside the box and solve challenges creatively, be logical but show excellence in thinking.

Don’t be a Malaka

It is a no BS rule at Xenia. Talk straight. Be honest. Build teamwork through ‘No triangles’.

What's On at Xenia

Live bouzouki, smashing plates, long lunches, dancing and pure Greek energy — Bouzoukia Sundays are back at Xenia, bringing the spirit of Greece to the Gold Coast each month.

Greek skewers, pita and tzatziki for $22 every Tuesday from 5pm. Casual, share-style dining done the Xenia way.

Celebrate New Year’s Eve at Xenia Main Beach or Coolangatta with a Greek banquet, live music, dancing, plate smashing and plenty of OPA.

Celebrate early with a Greek family feast at Xenia Main Beach or Coolangatta. Join us on New Year’s Eve from 4pm to 5.45pm, perfect for little ones. This seating is separate from our main NYE celebration. Menu and pricing coming soon.

Leave the cooking to us and enjoy the authentic flavours of Greece from the comfort of our own home. Our Xenia takeaway is here offering the best Takeaway in Main Beach & Coolangatta.

It's the locals event of the week and it's on every Thursday night! Enjoy a special Chef's Banquets with many Xenia favourite dishes and Happy Hour all night long. Available at Coolangatta and Main Beach every week.

Indulge the newlyweds, surprise your loved ones or reward your hardworking team - whoever it's for, trust us to take care of the experience.

functions venue Gold Coast

Events Venue Gold Coast

Xenia provides the perfect setting for events of all sizes, whether it's a milestone birthday, engagement party or a corporate function.

Nestled on the stunning Gold Coast, our two venues in Main Beach and Coolangatta embody Mediterranean charm, offering an authentic Greek ambiance.

Our exceptional food and beverage packages can be customised to your preferences and budget, while our expert catering and friendly staff ensure an unforgettable event!

FUNCTION & EVENT PACKAGES

Celebrate the Greek way at Xenia. From birthdays and long lunches to corporate celebrations, weddings and private events, we bring people together through generous feasting, warm hospitality and the true spirit of Greece.

Choose from shared banquets, canapé packages and drinks packages at Xenia Main Beach or Coolangatta, with private dining and full venue hire available to suit your celebration.

MAZI

Made for relaxed gatherings, team lunches and good company around the table.

To Start: Trio of homemade dips, pita, Greek olives and tiropita. Mains: Pork kalamakia, char-grilled chicken, lemon oregano fries, Aegean slaw and tzatziki.

TRAPEZI

Our most popular banquet, bringing together generous shared plates for a celebration the Greek way.

To Start: Trio of homemade dips, pita, Greek olives and saganaki cheese flamed with ouzo. Mezze: Flash-fried calamari, lemon and skordalia. Mains: Char-grilled chicken, slow-cooked lamb shoulder, lemonates patates, Greek salad and tzatziki.

PHILOXENIA

The complete Greek feast, made for special occasions and tables full of good company.

To Start: Trio of homemade dips, pita, Greek olives and grilled haloumi with sesame and sumac. Mezze: Chilli honey king prawn saganaki. Mains: Grilled meat platter of chicken skewers, lamb loin chops and wagyu and lamb biftekia, served with lemonates patates, Greek salad and tzatziki.

Add loukoumades to any banquet for +$4pp.

our event & FUNCTION spaces

Main Beach FUNCTION VENUE

Our Main Beach restaurant is a modern gem, ready for various events from cocktails to seated functions.

The alfresco area offers open-air dining, and our coastal-inspired restaurant and cozy lounges ensure a comfortable, intimate experience.

coolangatta FUNCTION VENUE
