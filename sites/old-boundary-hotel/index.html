The Old Boundary Hotel sits on Milroy Street in Bendigo, with a warm red brick interior and mining and gold rush memorabilia on every wall. Large red gum timber bars and high back stools await the thirsty ones, and open gas fires feature in both the bar and the lounge.

Call in and our bar staff and chefs will look after you with a cheery smile and the good old fashioned hospitality of yesteryear.

The Bar, Lounge and Terrace

Friendly staff will welcome you on arrival at the Lounge, which is separate from the bar and open 7 days a week.

At the back of the hotel you'll find the Terrace. It's a more relaxed space where the sun streams through the windows during the day, and in the evening the garden comes to light. The Terrace is fully air conditioned and heated.

Our Food

Our menu is built on a foundation of seasonal produce from local suppliers. We're committed to great value for money and a huge range of choice, with dishes created by our chefs in the kitchen. Dine in, take away or order delivery, with vegetarian options available.

On Tap and in the Cellar

A variety of ales, both heavy and light, are on tap. We pour beers from Carlton and United Breweries alongside a range of local craft beers, and our wine list includes a wide range of local varieties.
