YOMG Chermside is a burger and frozen yogurt spot in the Outdoor Dining Precinct at Westfield Chermside. Our burgers are house-made and hand-pressed on the grill with no additives. Our shakes are made with hand-scooped vanilla ice cream for the ultimate thickness.

Our frozen yogurt is hand-crafted using fresh milk and farmers yogurt. To keep things moving, our #YOMGlimited menu changes every two weeks. Dine in, grab takeaway or sit outside. Call us on (07) 3063 3799.

Vegan, Vegetarian and Dietary Info

Vegan and vegetarian items are marked across our menu. Miss Fu and The Vegan Hustle are vegan. Vegapalooza is vegetarian.

You can swap your protein for Plantein in your favourite YOMG burger, free of charge. You can also substitute our vegetarian fritter into any YOMG burger for free.

These items are also vegetarian:

  • Just Fries
  • Onion Rings
  • Cheese Fries
  • Chilli Cheese Fries
  • Nacho Fries
  • Mac & Cheese Bites

Our beef and chicken come from a halal-certified source. They are cooked on the same grill as bacon and prepared in the same kitchen as our pork products.

Allergens

Because of the risk of cross contamination, no food item at YOMG is sold as allergen-free. Our products contain or may contain traces of dairy, gluten, egg, soy, nuts, onion, garlic, sesame, pork and/or seafood. All products are prepared and handled on site, so we cannot guarantee that cross contamination will not occur.

Our frozen yogurt and toppings are self-serve, so we recommend that customers with allergies do not consume from this section. We do not have nutritional information available for our menu.

Ordering and Seating

We accept pre-ordered online pick-up orders. You can also order YOMG for delivery through Uber Eats, DoorDash or Menulog.

To book a table, visit obee.com.au/yomgchermside. If there are no free tables when you arrive, our friendly staff can put you on the waiting list and seat you at the next available table.

We are closed on Christmas Day.
