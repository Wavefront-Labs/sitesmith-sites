About

  • Takeaway

Sandobar is a takeaway and eat-in spot at Central Shopping Centre, 1 North Terrace, Bankstown. Locals come for burgers, sandwiches, fried rice and chicken schnitzel with chips and salad, served quickly by a warm, friendly team even when it's busy.

Pick your own salad from the fresh selection on display, including tabouli, and add gravy to your schnitzel for a little extra. After 4pm there's an afternoon deal, perfect for an easy dinner to take home. We're rated 4.8 from 83 Google reviews, and we appreciate every customer who takes the time to share their experience.
