Wraps of destiny! Exotic beverages. Bowls of deliciousness. Wrapture was born in the markets, tested on humans and perfected by the people, and we've been serving nourishment for everyone on Russell Street ever since.

We're a family business established in 2009, and we love to share tasty, nourishing food, all created here in our kitchen. Pull up a bright cushion, grab an outdoor table under the tree and take a break from the city pace.

Visit Wrapture

Find us at 71 Russell St, West End, Brisbane. Dine in among the bright cushions, sit outside, or grab your wrap to take away. Delivery is available too.
