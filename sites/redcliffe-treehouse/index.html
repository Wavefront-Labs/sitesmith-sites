About Redcliffe Treehouse

More than a café. More than a shop. A place where people come together.

Whether you're catching up with friends over coffee , discovering something special in our retail spaces, attending a community event, hiring a function room or simply looking for a welcoming place to spend some time, Redcliffe Treehouse has been created as a destination where everyone belongs.

Located in the heart of Redcliffe, we've brought together great coffee, thoughtful retail, flexible function spaces and a genuine sense of community under one roof, creating a place where people can enjoy great coffee, discover something new, connect with others and always find another reason to visit.

A Place Built Around Community

Redcliffe Treehouse was created with a simple idea in mind: build a space where people feel welcome.

From the moment you walk through our doors, you'll find more than somewhere to grab a coffee. You'll discover spaces designed to encourage conversation, creativity, learning and connection.

Whether you're visiting for a quiet coffee, searching for the perfect gift, exploring our book collection or planning your next celebration, you'll find there's always something new to discover at Redcliffe Treehouse.

More Than Your Typical Café

While great coffee and delicious food are part of every visit, Redcliffe Treehouse offers so much more.

Browse our carefully selected gift lines, fashion and books, discover products created by local makers, attend community events, hire one of our flexible function spaces or simply enjoy a relaxed afternoon in our welcoming surroundings.

Sometimes the best reason to visit isn't something you planned.

You're Invited to Stay

One of the things that makes Redcliffe Treehouse different is that you're invited to spend time here.

Play a tune on our community piano.

Challenge a friend to a board game.

Settle into one of our comfortable lounge spaces with a book.

Bring your laptop, settle in with a coffee and work remotely, or stay a little longer and enjoy lunch with us.

Or simply enjoy taking a break from a busy day.

We believe great spaces should encourage people to connect - not just with the place - but with each other.

Supporting Local

Community is at the heart of everything we do.

We're proud to support local makers, authors, creatives and small businesses by providing opportunities to showcase their work, connect with customers and become part of the Redcliffe Treehouse story.

As our community grows, so too will the experiences, events and partnerships available throughout the year.

Everyone is Welcome

Redcliffe Treehouse has been designed to be a welcoming and inclusive space for our entire community.

Our Ground Floor is wheelchair accessible, with step-free entry and an accessible bathroom, making it easier for everyone to enjoy what Treehouse has to offer.

Whether you're visiting for five minutes or a few hours, you'll always be welcomed with a smile.

The Family Behind Redcliffe Treehouse

Every welcoming place has a story, and Redcliffe Treehouse is no exception.

Created by Cei Heck and brought to life with the support of her family, Redcliffe Treehouse has always been about more than opening the doors to a café or retail space. It was built around a belief that everyone deserves somewhere they feel welcome, supported and connected.

Together with her husband Barrie, whose practical skills helped transform the vision into reality, and her daughter Abi, who continues to play an important role in daily business, the family has created a place that reflects the values they live every day: kindness, flexibility, inclusion and community.

Those values continue to shape every decision made at the Treehouse, from creating welcoming spaces where people feel comfortable staying a little longer, to supporting local makers, authors, community groups and small businesses.

For the family, Redcliffe Treehouse isn't simply somewhere to visit. It's somewhere they hope people will feel they belong.

A Different Kind of Place to Gather

Your event doesn't need to fit a traditional function room.

Meet With a View

Upstairs, the Moreton Room offers something completely different, with natural light and views across Moreton Bay.

Private When You Need It

Accessible Meetings & Training

Need a private space where everyone can participate?

Breakfast, Lunch & Great Coffee

Whether you're joining us for breakfast with friends, a relaxed lunch, your favourite coffee or booking a special High Tea celebration, every visit to The Redcliffe Treehouse is an opportunity to slow down and enjoy good food in great company. Our menu changes with the seasons, celebrating fresh ingredients and homemade favourites.

Coffee Your Way

Everyone enjoys coffee a little differently, which is why we offer a variety of milk options to suit different tastes and dietary preferences.

Whether you're a flat white regular or trying something new, we'll help you enjoy your coffee just the way you like it.

Sweet Treats & Gelati

There’s always something delicious waiting for you in our cake cabinet! Enjoy a selection of freshly baked sweet treats all made in-house, alongside premium gelati including dairy free, gluten free and vegan-friendly options, making it easy for everyone to find a favourite.

Dietary Requirements

We believe everyone deserves to feel welcome around the table.

Whether you're joining us for breakfast, lunch, coffee or catering, we understand how important it is to feel confident that your dietary requirements can be accommodated. We offer gluten free, dairy free, vegetarian and coeliac-friendly options daily, along with dairy free, gluten free and vegan gelati flavours.

Our baristas also stock all mainstream dairy and plant-based milk alternatives, with the exception of macadamia milk, making it easy to enjoy your favourite coffee just the way you like it.

For catering bookings, we're happy to discuss vegan, Low FODMAP and diabetic-friendly requirements where possible. While most of our catering options are nut free, some menu items may contain nuts. If you have food allergies or specific dietary requirements, simply let our team know when ordering and we'll do our very best to accommodate your needs.
