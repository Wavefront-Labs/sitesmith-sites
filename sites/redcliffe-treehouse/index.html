The Redcliffe Treehouse is more than a café. It's a place where people come together, with great coffee, fresh food, books, gifts, fashion and flexible function spaces under one roof at 35 Redcliffe Parade.

It started when a seed of an idea was planted: find a cosy spot to grab a cuppa and play board games with fab friends. From there it grew, with input, ideas and buckets of hard work from so many wonderful people, into a community hub where you're invited to stay a little longer.

Menu

Visit Our Café

Join us for your morning coffee, a relaxed breakfast or lunch, a catch-up with friends, a client meeting or a few hours working remotely. Food is prepared onsite, and our menu changes with the seasons, celebrating fresh ingredients and homemade favourites.

Sweet Treats & Gelati

There's always something delicious in our cake cabinet, with freshly baked sweet treats all made in-house, alongside premium gelati including dairy free, gluten free and vegan-friendly options.

Dietary Requirements

  • Gluten free, dairy free, vegetarian and coeliac-friendly options daily
  • Dairy free, gluten free and vegan gelati flavours
  • All mainstream dairy and plant-based milks, except macadamia milk

A Place to Slow Down

Browse our book collection, challenge friends to a board game, enjoy live music from our community piano or play it yourself. Catch up on work with free Wi-Fi, or simply settle in with a coffee and watch the world go by.

Our ground floor is wheelchair accessible, with step-free entry and an accessible bathroom. To book a table, call us on 07 2004 0323.

Fancy a High Tea?

We can provide High Tea for your friends and family, in groups small or large, so why not indulge? We have views to Moreton Island, perfect for whale watching season.

  • Gluten free options are available
  • Dairy free and vegan options require 48 hours notice

To enquire and book, call us on 07 2004 0323.

Function & Venue Hire

Meet. Work. Learn. Connect. Celebrate. Beyond our busy café you'll find public and private spaces for meetings, workshops, training, networking, celebrations and private gatherings. We didn't want The Redcliffe Treehouse to feel like a traditional function centre.

  • The Moreton Room (upstairs): natural light, views across Moreton Bay and flexible furniture for workshops, business gatherings, celebrations and catered events
  • The Board Room (upstairs): a private setting for smaller meetings, with a dedicated boardroom table and corporate seating
  • The Training Room (ground floor): flexible seating for meetings, workshops and training, with step-free access, an accessible bathroom nearby and a wide custom doorway for oversized electric wheelchairs
  • The Lounge, Gathering Table and shared spaces: for informal conversations, smaller celebrations, networking and relaxed connection

Your event doesn't have to stay in one room. Upstairs, the Moreton Room and Break Out Room can create different zones within the one event.

Catering

Having our café right here means catering isn't an afterthought. We offer freshly prepared catering for business meetings, community gatherings, family celebrations and private events, made with the same rustic, home-style approach you'll find in our café.

We're happy to discuss vegan, Low FODMAP and diabetic-friendly requirements where possible. Most of our catering options are nut free, but some menu items may contain nuts, so please let our team know about any allergies when ordering.

Books, Gifts & Boutique

Bookstore

Not a book exchange or library, our bookstore is committed to fostering literacy across the community with an extensive and diverse selection. Supporting local authors is high on our list, so come and launch your latest book at The Redcliffe Treehouse. Hot tip: this is where you'll find the comfy couches and board games.

Gift Shop

Discover a curated collection of gifts, home décor, games and locally made treasures, for birthdays, thank-yous, new babies or just for you. Every product has been chosen because it brings beauty, practicality or joy into everyday life. And because we can, gift wrapping is available.

If you're a maker, artist or small business with products you think would suit the Treehouse, we'd love to hear from you.

Boutique

The Redcliffe Treehouse is kind of like a DFO but with a view. When gorgeous stores and brands pull up stumps, we grab the goods so you can grab a bargain. We have the last ever remaining stock from Girl And The Sun, who are no longer manufacturing after 12 years of trade, and from Mulholland Drive from the Gold Coast, including the black pants with sequins.

The Family Behind the Treehouse

Redcliffe Treehouse was created by Cei Heck and brought to life with the support of her family, including her husband Barrie, whose practical skills helped transform the vision into reality, and her daughter Abi. It was built on a belief that everyone deserves somewhere they feel welcome, supported and connected.

We're proud to support local makers, authors, creatives and small businesses. As our community grows, so too will the experiences, events and partnerships available throughout the year. Whether you're visiting for five minutes or a few hours, you'll always be welcomed with a smile.
