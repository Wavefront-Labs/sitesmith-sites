The Viet cafe is a Vietnamese restaurant at 100 Brisbane Rd, Labrador. Come in and dine with us, or order delivery and enjoy it at home.

Our spicy beef soup keeps regulars coming back, and our beef pho has been praised by diners who've eaten it in Vietnam. We also pour a bold, strong coffee, all served with friendly service.
