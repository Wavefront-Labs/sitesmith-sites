Nero's Table offers an affordable and relaxed Mediterranean dining experience with wood-fired pizzas, pastas and a full bar in a warm, welcoming atmosphere. You'll find us at 58 Bull St, Bendigo.

We're open from 11:30am every day except Tuesday. Dine in, grab takeaway or sit outside, and there are plenty of vegetarian options on the menu.

Bottomless Beverage Package

Two hours of shared food and drinks for $69 per person, available Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Sessions start at either 11:30am or 12:30pm and run for 2 hours from your confirmed booking time.

Shared food may include

  • Shared breads with house dips
  • Charcuterie selection
  • Chef's selection of bites
  • Shared dessert plate

Menu items may vary depending on seasonal availability.

Drinks during your session

  • Mimosas
  • Lemon or Blood Orange Spritz
  • Bubbly
  • Pink Moscato
  • House wines
  • Selected beers on tap

Good to know

  • Bookings are essential and full prepayment is required. Walk-ins cannot be guaranteed.
  • Minimum booking of two guests, and the entire table must take part in the package.
  • One drink per guest at a time. The next is served once the previous one is finished.
  • Last drinks are called 10 minutes before the end of the session. Late arrivals do not extend the session.
  • Bookings may be released if guests arrive more than 15 minutes late.
  • Cancellations or changes must be made at least 24 hours before the booking. Late cancellations or no-shows may forfeit payment.
  • Please tell us about dietary requirements when booking. We can't guarantee an allergen-free environment.
  • We follow Victorian Responsible Service of Alcohol laws. Service may be refused or ended, with no refund, where guests are intoxicated or behave inappropriately.
  • Cannot be combined with other promotions or discounts. Limited or unavailable during special events and public holidays, and may be amended or withdrawn without notice.
