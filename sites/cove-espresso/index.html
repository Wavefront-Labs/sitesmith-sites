About

  • Dine in
  • Takeaway

Cove Espresso is your newest coffee hotspot in Caloundra's Daniel Street industrial area, at 25 Enterprise St, Caloundra West. Sip on delicious coffee, savour tasty sandwiches and indulge in sweet treats, all from our cosy coffee van.

Join us for a quick caffeine fix or a leisurely break from the day. Dine in or grab it to take away. It's well worth the detour on your drive to work.

Good to know

Are your sandwiches made fresh daily?

Yes, all our sandwiches are made fresh each morning using locally sourced ingredients.

Can I customise my coffee order?

Of course! Let our baristas know your preferences, and we'll make your coffee just the way you like it.
