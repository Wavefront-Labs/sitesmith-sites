Berriko's Dandenong is our flagship diner and the one that never closes. It serves the full menu all day and all night, from 3pm coffee runs to 4am burger stops, with dine-in, takeaway and delivery.

We're a Melbourne-owned, family-run restaurant group that started in 2018 as a small dessert bar. Benedict and big breakfasts in the morning, mezze and pasta at lunch, murgh makhani and risotto at night. We don't pick a lane, we do each one properly, and every plate is made to order and served to your table.

Made to order. Every order.

Nothing sits under a heat lamp. Every crêpe is poured to order, every burger patty smashed when you pay, every waffle pressed while your cream is being whipped.

It's slower than a chain. It's also the whole point: food you can tell was actually cooked for you. Takeaway's there if you need it, but we designed this for sitting down.

We started with one warm brownie

Berriko's opened in 2018 as a small dessert bar, with one kitchen, a short menu, and the kind of loaded waffles and Nutella crêpes that people started sharing on their phones before we could get their coffee to the table. We only wanted to do a few things well: a warm brownie that tasted the way brownies are supposed to taste, a pour-over coffee that wasn't an afterthought, and desserts that felt like a night out.

Then customers came for the Dark Obsession at 10pm and stayed asking for something to eat before dessert. So we put burgers on the menu. Then grilled melts. Then a proper souvlaki and a few Indian-leaning dishes that don't hold back on spice. Before we planned it, Berriko's had become an all-day diner: coffee in the morning, a burger at midnight, and a brownie at 2am.

Berriko's is independently Australian-owned and operated. You'll usually find one of us on the floor, in the kitchen or behind the counter. That's the only way we know how to keep every one of our restaurants running to the same standard.

Franchise with Berriko's

We're opening applications for Berriko's franchisees across Australia: a dessert-forward, all-day diner concept with a proven model and the support to open a world-class venue.
