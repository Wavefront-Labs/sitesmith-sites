Jamie's Kitchen Coffee & Food is a friendly Waitara spot at 1 Park Lane, near Hornsby, serving Campos coffee, all-day breakfast, generous brunch plates, fresh drinks, hearty lunches and relaxed dinners. It's low key and welcoming, whether you're dropping in for a quick flat white or settling in with family for dinner.

Behind every plate is a professional café kitchen, where our skilled chefs prepare many sauces and dishes in house. That care makes lunch and dinner just as important to us as coffee and brunch.
