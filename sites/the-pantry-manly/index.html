The Pantry Manly serves modern Australian food with subtle Mediterranean influences, made with fresh, locally sourced ingredients and set right on the edge of one of Australia's most iconic beaches. Start the day with breakfast on our ground floor, just steps from the sand, or settle in for a light all-day menu that suits a leisurely lunch or a casual bite by the beach.

For something more special, our first-floor restaurant has panoramic views across Manly Beach for lunch or dinner all year round. Relax over good food and wine while you watch the surfers, the sun on the water and, if you're lucky, dolphins playing or whales passing along the coastline.

Our History

Manly's famous Beach House was built in 1919 and spent many years as a tea house and change rooms for locals and tourists alike. In 1982 it became an information kiosk, and in 1997 it became a restaurant, remembered as Manly Ocean Beach House.

Renamed The Pantry Manly and opened in November 2010, it carries on the tradition of looking after Manly locals and international visitors, who can sit back, relax and take in the real Manly vibe.
