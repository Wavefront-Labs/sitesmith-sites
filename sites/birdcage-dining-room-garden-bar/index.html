Birthday Bash

Your Birthday Meal is on Us!

Birthdays are meant to be celebrated, and at Birdcage we’re making them even sweeter.

Gather your favourite people and book a table for 6 or more guests. As the birthday host, you’ll receive a complimentary birthday meal package valued at up to $60.

How It Works

  • Book a table for 6 or more guests.
  • Mention the Birthday Offer when booking online (in the booking notes) or over the phone.
  • Each guest must purchase one main meal and one beverage.
  • The offer is valid during your birthday month.
  • Present a valid photo ID or government-issued identification upon arrival.
  • Groups of 15 or more guests must book through our Events Manager and dine from the function menu. Please email events@tableone.com.au .

Ready to celebrate? Book your birthday gathering at Birdcage and let us take care of the birthday meal.

Terms & Conditions

One complimentary birthday meal package per booking. The package includes one main meal (up to $45 value) and one beverage (up to $15 value). The offer is available only for bookings of 6 or more guests where each guest purchases one main meal and one beverage. Valid during the birthday guest’s birthday month. A valid photo ID or government-issued identification must be presented on arrival. Not available in conjunction with any other offer unless otherwise stated. Management reserves the right to amend or withdraw this offer at any time.
