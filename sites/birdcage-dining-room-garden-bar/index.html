The Dining Room

Welcome to our dining room, where memories are created and culinary bliss is served with a side of pleasure. Expect modern Australian food built around premium steaks and fresh seafood, made for sharing.

Dine in, grab takeaway or sit outside, with vegetarian options available. Bookings are welcome.

A 10% surcharge applies on Saturdays and Sundays. A 15% surcharge applies on public holidays.

The Garden Bar

Welcome to The Garden Bar, our indoor and outdoor oasis. We've transformed the space next door into a cosy, intimate bar, perfect for sipping cocktails or crisp tap beers under a canopy of fairy-lit trees.

With bar tables and a relaxed vibe, it's made for catching up with friends, meeting neighbours or a casual date night. Our premium bar menu is designed for sharing, or choose your favourite dishes from the Birdcage dining menu.

We show live sports every week on the big screen, including AFL, NRL and more. The atmosphere is warm, the drinks are flowing, the outdoor heaters are on and the vibe is just right.

Your Birthday Meal is on Us

Birthdays are meant to be celebrated, and we're making them even sweeter. Book a table for 6 or more guests and, as the birthday host, you'll receive a complimentary birthday meal package valued at up to $60.

How it works

  • Book a table for 6 or more guests.
  • Mention the Birthday Offer when booking online (in the booking notes) or over the phone.
  • Each guest must purchase one main meal and one beverage.
  • The offer is valid during your birthday month.
  • Present a valid photo ID or government-issued identification on arrival.
  • Groups of 15 or more guests must book through our Events Manager and dine from the function menu. Please email events@tableone.com.au.

Terms and conditions

One complimentary birthday meal package per booking. The package includes one main meal (up to $45 value) and one beverage (up to $15 value). The offer is available only for bookings of 6 or more guests where each guest purchases one main meal and one beverage. Valid during the birthday guest's birthday month. A valid photo ID or government-issued identification must be presented on arrival. Not available in conjunction with any other offer unless otherwise stated. Management reserves the right to amend or withdraw this offer at any time.

Christmas Functions

Make this Christmas one to remember at Birdcage in the heart of Main Beach. Gather your colleagues, friends or family and celebrate the festive season with delicious food, great drinks and wonderful company.
