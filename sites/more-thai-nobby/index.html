Experience the authentic taste of Thailand with our delicious dishes made from the freshest ingredients. Come and visit us to More Thai began with a simple vision — to share authentic Thai flavours with the warmth of genuine hospitality. From humble beginnings in Mermaid Beach, we’ve grown into three vibrant Gold Coast locations, guided by passion, family recipes, and a love for bringing people together.
