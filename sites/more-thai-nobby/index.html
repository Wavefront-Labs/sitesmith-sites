More Thai began with a simple vision: to share authentic Thai flavours with the warmth of genuine hospitality. From humble beginnings in Mermaid Beach, we've grown into three vibrant Gold Coast locations, guided by passion, family recipes and a love for bringing people together.

Led by Luke, Wendy and Nan, every dish is crafted with care, tradition and heart. More Thai is more than a restaurant. It's a place where flavour meets community.

Our Kitchen

Our chef

With 20 years of experience cooking in Thai restaurants, our chef is excited to present their vision to you and all our guests. Our caring and committed staff, our family, make sure you have a fantastic experience with us.

Our ingredients

We believe the quality of our food starts with the quality of our ingredients. That's why we source our produce, meat and seafood from local farms and suppliers whenever possible, and use traditional Thai herbs and spices to give our dishes their signature flavour. Come taste the difference.
