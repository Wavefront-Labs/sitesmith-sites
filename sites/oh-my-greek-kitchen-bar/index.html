Oh My Greek Kitchen & Bar brings all the flavours of the Greek Islands down under to Hope Island. Our menu is fuelled by flavour and memories, combining traditional flavours, fresh, high-quality local ingredients and the simplicity of home cooking.

We invite you to sit back, relax and choose your favourite dishes. As the Greeks say, "Kali orexi": happy eating!
