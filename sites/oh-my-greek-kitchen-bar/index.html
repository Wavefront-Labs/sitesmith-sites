Oh My Greek is the newest, most exciting Greek restaurant located in Hope Island that brings all the flavours of the Greek Islands down under. The menu is fueled by flavour and memories, combining traditional flavours, fresh high quality local ingredients, and the simplicity of home cooking. We invite you to sit back and relax. Select your favourite dishes from our menu. To compliment your meal, our wine list consist of several excellent Greek wines and beers. As a perfect finish, don’t forget our array of tempting Greek desserts! As the Greeks say “Kali orexi” – Happy Eating
