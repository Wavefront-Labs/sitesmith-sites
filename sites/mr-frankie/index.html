Created for the locals by the locals, Mr Frankie is a family owned cafe bringing a little slice of Melbourne to Frankston South. We opened in May 2019 in what was once the old news agency on Norman Avenue, transformed into an architecturally designed fit out with real attention to detail.

Quality sourced produce, top of the range coffee from Industry Beans, locally sourced wine and warm, friendly service make this a special place for locals to enjoy and to proudly bring their friends from all over.
