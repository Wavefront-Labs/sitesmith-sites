BANZAI

D iscover Our Story ✻ “Banzai” is a Japanese cheer that translates as “Long life!” or “Hurrah!” It is usually repeated three times to express enthusiasm, celebrate a victory, applause and favour on happy occasion while raising both arms. It is commonly done together with the large group of people.
